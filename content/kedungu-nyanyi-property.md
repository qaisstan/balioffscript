Kedungu and Nyanyi sit on the Tabanan coast just north of Seseh, and they are where the corridor's northward march currently stops.

## What is happening there

Nyanyi hosts the Nuanu development, a large master-planned project that has brought infrastructure, attention and a concentration of new accommodation to a stretch that had almost none.

Kedungu, slightly further north, has a surf beach, a river mouth, rice fields and a growing number of villas.

This is the most visible example in Bali of a corridor being created rather than grown.

## The investment case

**Price.** Tabanan pricing, which is a fraction of Canggu, for coastline in the same growth path.

**Catalyst.** A large master-planned development nearby does change an area, and this one has.

**Surf.** Kedungu is a genuine break, which anchors a certain kind of demand independent of cafes.

## The risk

Demand here is currently produced by the development, not by the area. That is a meaningful distinction.

If the masterplan delivers at scale, the surrounding land benefits. If it slows, you own a villa in Tabanan with Tabanan rental demand and a Canggu-adjacent purchase price.

Buy land on Tabanan fundamentals and treat the development as upside, rather than paying for the upside up front.

## What to verify

- Zoning on the parcel. Most surrounding land is agricultural
- Access road width and condition
- Water supply and monthly cost in full operation
- Drive time to Canggu and the airport at real hours
- How much accommodation is under construction within two kilometres

## Who it suits

Long-horizon buyers who want coastline at Tabanan prices and can wait for demand to arrive. Not buyers needing income in year one.

## Common questions

### Where is Kedungu?
On the Tabanan coast north of Seseh and Cemagi, roughly 25 to 40 minutes from Canggu depending on traffic.

### Is Nyanyi a good place to invest?
It has a genuine catalyst in the nearby master-planned development. Price the land on Tabanan fundamentals and treat the development as upside rather than paying for it in advance.

### How much is land in Kedungu?
Tabanan pricing, substantially below Pererenan and Canggu, with wide variation by zoning and access.

### Is there rental demand in Kedungu?
Growing but still thin compared with the corridor to the south, and currently driven by nearby development rather than by the area itself.

### Is Kedungu good for surfing?
Yes, it is an established break, which supports a different and more weather-dependent demand pattern than cafe-driven areas.
