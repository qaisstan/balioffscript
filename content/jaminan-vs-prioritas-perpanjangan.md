This is the single most consequential pair of words in a Bali leasehold, and most foreign buyers never see them because the lease is in Indonesian.

## The two phrases

**Jaminan perpanjangan** means a **guarantee** of extension. The landowner is bound to extend on the terms stated.

**Prioritas perpanjangan** means **priority** for extension. You get first refusal. The landowner is not bound to extend at all, only to come to you first if they decide to.

Those are not variations of the same clause. One is a right. The other is a queue position.

## Why it matters at exactly the wrong moment

Your extension falls due after twenty-five or thirty years, by which time you have built a villa on the land, furnished it, run it, and cannot move it.

With **jaminan**, the landowner must extend on the stated terms.

With **prioritas**, they can decline, or name a number, and your alternative is walking away from a building you paid for. That is not a negotiation, it is a hold-up, and both sides know it.

## What to insist on

| Element | What good looks like |
| --- | --- |
| The word | Jaminan perpanjangan, not prioritas |
| Extension term | A stated number of years |
| Price | A figure, or a formula tied to a published index |
| Notice | A defined window, with a consequence for late notice that is not forfeiture |
| Successors | Binds heirs and any future owner of the land |

"Extendable at market rate" is the English-language equivalent of prioritas. It reads as a right and functions as the landowner's option.

## Read the Indonesian, not the translation

Bali leases are executed in Indonesian. English versions circulated by agents are often summaries rather than translations, and this distinction is exactly the kind of nuance a summary loses.

Have your own notary read the Indonesian text and confirm, in writing, which phrase appears.

## If your existing lease says prioritas

You are not without options, but you are negotiating from a weaker position than you thought. Raise it early, well before the term runs down, when the landowner still values the relationship and your leverage has not yet evaporated.

Waiting until year twenty-eight is the worst possible time to discover this.

## Common questions

### What is jaminan perpanjangan?
Guarantee of extension. The landowner is bound to extend on the terms stated in the lease.

### What is prioritas perpanjangan?
Priority for extension, meaning first refusal only. The landowner is not obliged to extend at all.

### Which should be in my Bali lease?
Jaminan. Priority is not an extension right and it leaves you exposed at the moment your building cannot be moved.

### Is "extendable at market rate" the same problem?
Yes, in English. It leaves the price to be set when your alternative is abandoning the building.

### What if my lease already says prioritas?
Raise it early rather than near expiry. Your leverage is highest while the term still has years to run.
