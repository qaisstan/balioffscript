PBB is the recurring tax on owning property in Indonesia, and the number is usually small enough that people forget it exists until it causes a problem.

## How it works

**Pajak Bumi dan Bangunan** is charged annually on land and any building on it, based on **NJOP**, the government's assessed value.

NJOP is set by the authorities and updated periodically. It is typically below actual market value, which is why the tax lands lower than foreign owners expect.

## What it costs

Modest relative to property taxes in most Western countries. For a typical Bali villa it is a manageable annual figure rather than a meaningful cost of ownership.

That is one of the genuine advantages of holding property here: Indonesia taxes transactions and income rather than the fact of owning.

## Why the receipts matter more than the amount

**Unpaid PBB surfaces at sale.** A buyer's notary will ask for the receipts. Gaps create delay at exactly the point you are trying to close.

**It is evidence of the ownership chain.** Continuous PBB payment in a consistent name supports the title history.

**It can affect other processes.** Permit applications and licence matters can require evidence that property taxes are current.

So collect the receipts, keep them with the certificate, and when you buy, ask the seller for several years of them. Gaps are worth understanding before you take the property on.

## Who pays it on a leasehold

The lease should say. On many Bali leases the landowner remains liable as the registered holder while the lessee reimburses, but leaving it unstated creates an argument.

Get it in the document: who pays, who holds the receipts, and what happens if it goes unpaid.

## NJOP as a valuation reference

Because NJOP is public and assessed, it is sometimes used as a sanity check on price. Treat it as a floor indicator rather than a valuation. It lags the market, often substantially, particularly in fast-moving corridors.

## Common questions

### What is PBB in Indonesia?
Pajak Bumi dan Bangunan, the annual tax on land and buildings, based on the government's assessed NJOP value.

### How much is property tax in Bali?
Modest by Western standards, because it is calculated on NJOP rather than market value. It is not a significant cost of ownership.

### What is NJOP?
The government's assessed value of a property, used for PBB and as the floor for BPHTB calculation.

### Who pays PBB on a leased property?
Whoever the lease says. The registered holder is liable, so the lease should state who actually bears and pays it.

### What happens if PBB is unpaid?
It accrues and surfaces at sale, when a buyer's notary asks for the receipts. Keep them with the certificate.
