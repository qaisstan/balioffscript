The legal framework is identical because it is national law. Everything commercial is different.

## The comparison

| | Bali | Lombok |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Land price | High and rising in the corridors | A fraction |
| Build cost | Low, deep contractor pool | Lower, fewer skilled contractors |
| Tourism volume | The regional benchmark | A small share of Bali's |
| International flights | Extensive | Limited |
| Rental demand | Mature, foreign-operated villas | Thin, concentrated in the south |
| Resale pool | Deep in the main corridors | Shallow |
| Title irregularity | Present, manageable | More common |
| Professional services | Well established | Thinner |

## The Lombok case

South Lombok around Kuta, Selong Belanak and Mawun has genuine surf, genuine beauty and land at prices Bali passed fifteen years ago.

The Mandalika special economic zone was meant to be the catalyst, anchored by the MotoGP circuit and planned resort development. Some has been built. A good deal has not, on the original timeline or scale.

That is the pattern to price for. Buy on what exists, not on a masterplan.

## The Bali case

Demand that already exists, an airport with international connectivity, a mature villa rental economy, and an exit market with actual buyers in it.

You pay for all of that, and in the oversupplied corridors you can pay too much.

## The honest question

**Are you buying income or buying time?**

Bali produces income now, at a price that reflects it. Lombok is priced for growth that has not arrived, and may arrive slowly.

Both are legitimate. Confusing them is not. A Lombok purchase underwritten on Bali occupancy assumptions will disappoint, and a Bali purchase made because Lombok felt too slow usually means overpaying for the generic product.

## What to verify in Lombok specifically

Title history, which is more often irregular, particularly on land passed through inheritance without registration. Access rights, registered rather than customary. Zoning. And realistically, who buys it from you in five years.

## Common questions

### Is Lombok cheaper than Bali?
Considerably, for both land and construction, reflecting much lower tourism volume and thinner infrastructure.

### Is Lombok a good investment?
It is a bet on future demand rather than existing demand. That suits capital you do not need back for a decade.

### Can foreigners buy property in Lombok?
Yes, under the same national framework as Bali: leasehold, Hak Pakai with residency, or HGB through a PT PMA.

### Did Mandalika deliver?
Partially. Treat masterplan projections as intentions rather than facts and price the land on what exists today.

### Is Lombok the next Bali?
It has been described that way for two decades. Flight connectivity and tourism volume remain a fraction of Bali's.
