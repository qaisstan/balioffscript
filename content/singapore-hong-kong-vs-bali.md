Singapore and Hong Kong are where regional capital is parked. Bali is where some of it goes to be spent.

## The ownership position

**Singapore.** Foreigners can buy condominiums freely. Landed residential property is restricted and requires approval. The barrier is not permission, it is **Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty**, which for foreign buyers is severe and deliberately so.

**Hong Kong.** Foreigners can buy, subject to stamp duties that have been used as a cooling tool for years, though some were relaxed as the market softened.

**Bali.** No ownership at all for foreigners. Lease, Hak Pakai or HGB through a company.

## What that actually means

Singapore and Hong Kong do not stop you owning. They price you out with duty. Indonesia does not charge you much and does not let you own.

Those are different systems producing a similar outcome: the foreign buyer holds less than a local does.

## Why Singapore money buys in Bali

Not as an alternative to Singapore property. As a different asset class entirely.

A Singapore-based buyer is not choosing between a Singapore condominium and a Canggu villa. They already have the first and want the second for cash flow, lifestyle, or both, at an entry price that is a rounding error against Singapore prices.

That is why the flow exists and why it is relatively insensitive to Bali's ownership restrictions. The capital is not looking for security. It has security at home.

## What it means for you

If you are choosing Bali *instead of* a secure market because you cannot afford the secure one, you are taking the risk for the wrong reason.

If you are adding Bali to something stable, the weaker rights matter less because they are not carrying your whole position.

## Common questions

### Can foreigners buy property in Singapore?
Condominiums yes, landed residential is restricted. The real barrier is Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty, which is high for foreign buyers.

### Can foreigners buy property in Hong Kong?
Yes, subject to stamp duties that have long been used to cool the market.

### Why do Singaporeans buy property in Bali?
For cash flow and lifestyle at an entry price that is trivial against Singapore prices, usually in addition to holdings at home rather than instead of them.

### Is Bali cheaper than Singapore for property?
By an enormous margin, which is much of the attraction. You are also buying a term-limited right rather than ownership.

### Which is the safer investment?
Singapore and Hong Kong on rule of law, liquidity and currency. Bali on yield, with considerably more risk carried by you.
