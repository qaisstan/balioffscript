Bali and Phuket get compared more than any other pair in Asia, and for good reason. Both are islands with an international airport, a villa market built on holiday rentals and a big foreign crowd. The difference is on paper, and it decides almost everything.

## What a foreigner can actually own

**Phuket.** You can own a **condominium unit freehold, in your own name**, as long as the building's foreign quota has room. Foreigners together may hold at most **49% of the floor area** of a building. When the quota is full, no more freehold units go to foreigners in that building. Land cannot be owned by a foreigner. Villas are therefore bought on a **registered lease of up to 30 years**, often with promised renewals that are not guaranteed by law.

**Bali.** You cannot own land or a villa in your own name. You buy a **lease (Hak Sewa)**, commonly 25 to 30 years with an extension option, or use **Hak Pakai** with residency, or **HGB through a PT PMA** company. There is no freehold for foreigners.

So Phuket wins on one thing outright: a condo you own forever. Bali wins on another: villa leases are often longer in practice, because the extension is written into the deal from day one.

## Registered vs unregistered

A Thai lease is **registered at the Land Department** and shows on the title deed. An Indonesian lease is a contract between you and the owner, usually signed before a notary, but not registered on the land certificate. That makes the Bali due diligence more important: who really owns the land, whether every heir signed, whether the extension price is fixed. That is exactly the work most Bali buyers skip.

## Rental market and yields

Both islands live on short-term holiday letting, with high gross yields and high running costs.

- **Bali** has more demand spread over more areas (Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud, Seseh, Sanur) and a longer list of source markets: Australia, Europe, Asia. A well-run villa nets roughly **7 to 14%** depending on quality and management.
- **Phuket** has a stronger condo market, deeper management companies and more hotel-branded residences. Villa rentals are more seasonal, with a pronounced monsoon low season.

Bali's edge is yield on villas. Phuket's edge is a simpler, registered asset you can sell to the next foreigner without a lease running down.

## Resale

A Phuket freehold condo does not lose years. A Bali lease does: a 30-year lease with 18 years left is worth much less than when it was new. If you buy in Bali, buy with a **long term and a fixed extension**, or plan to hold for income rather than resale.

## Visas and living there

Thailand's **Long-Term Resident** and **Thailand Privilege** visas are established and predictable. Indonesia's **second home visa** and **golden visa** are newer and still settling. If living there full time matters more than income, Thailand is easier today.

## Which I would choose

- **You want to own something outright and sell it easily later:** a condo in Phuket, in a building with quota left.
- **You want a villa that pays you, in a place you will use:** Bali, with a long lease, a written extension and a proper check of the land and the owner before you pay.
- **You want both:** that is a budget question, not a country question.

## Common questions

### Can foreigners buy a villa in Phuket?
Not the land. A Phuket villa is usually held on a registered 30-year lease, sometimes with a company structure for the building. Promised extensions beyond 30 years are contract promises, not registered rights.

### Is Bali or Phuket cheaper?
It depends on the area and the product. Entry-level Bali villas are often cheaper than Phuket villas with sea views, while Phuket condos can be cheaper than Bali villas. Compare the price per year of lease you actually get, not just the sticker price.

### Which has higher rental yields?
Bali villas, on average, with more management work and more risk. Phuket condos give lower but steadier returns.

### Did Thailand pass the 99-year lease?
No. As of late 2026, the maximum registered lease is still 30 years. The 99-year proposal and a higher condo quota have been discussed but not enacted.

### Is it safer to buy in Phuket?
The title system is more transparent, because leases are registered. Bali is safe when the paperwork is checked properly; most bad stories come from buyers who skipped the checks.
