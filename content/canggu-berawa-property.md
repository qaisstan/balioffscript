Canggu is where roughly a third of Bali's transactions happen. That liquidity is the product you are buying — not the yield.

Berawa land runs around **USD 82,500 per are** (100 m²) on leasehold. Rates have been compressing as supply grows, and traffic is now a real constraint on guest experience rather than a joke about the shortcut.

## What actually breaks deals here

Badung is the strictest regency in Bali on short-term rental enforcement, and Canggu is where enforcement lands first.

The common trap is simple: a villa is operating, generating income, and has **no PBG and no SLF**. It has been running for years. The seller treats that history as proof it is fine.

It is not. Past operation is not evidence of current compliance. When a new owner or a new company takes over, the licensing position is assessed fresh — against today's rules, not the ones that applied when the villa was built.

## Zoning

Much of residential Canggu is yellow. Yellow does not automatically permit commercial accommodation. Some activity may be conditionally permitted depending on the specific RDTR for that plot — but "conditionally permitted" is a finding, not an assumption, and it varies street by street.

> A villa operating next door proves nothing about your plot. Each parcel and each licence stands on its own.

## Permit timelines, realistically

Badung is the slowest regency in Bali for permits, because it has the highest volume of tourism projects and the most intensive field inspections.

| Situation | Realistic timeline |
| --- | --- |
| PBG from empty land | 5–6 months |
| PBG + SLF for an existing unpermitted building | 10–12 months |

Budget that time and cost into any deal where permits are incomplete. It is not a formality you resolve after closing.

## What to check before you pay anything

- PBG exists for every structure on the plot, and SLF has been issued
- The RDTR designation for this specific parcel, obtained yourself, not from the agent
- Whether the intended rental model matches an available business classification
- Setbacks — beach, river, and any temple buffer
- Whether the operating company's licence covers this location, not just its domicile

If the yield projection assumes nightly rental, the licensing question comes before the numbers. Run the numbers anyway — the [ROI calculator](/calculator/) will show you what Canggu actually returns after costs.
