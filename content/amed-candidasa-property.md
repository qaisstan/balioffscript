Amed and Candidasa sit on Bali's east coast in Karangasem, facing Lombok across the strait.

## What is there

**Amed** is a string of fishing villages along a black sand coastline, built around diving and snorkelling. The USAT Liberty wreck at nearby Tulamben is one of the most dived sites in Asia.

**Candidasa** is an older resort town with reasonable infrastructure, a quieter and more faded feel, and low prices.

Both sit under Mount Agung, with the volcano dominating the skyline.

## The numbers

Land here is a fraction of the southern corridors and often below Tabanan. For the price of a small Canggu plot you buy something with a sea view and a volcano behind it.

That is genuine, and it is priced that way for reasons.

## The limitations

**Rental demand is thin and seasonal.** Divers come for a few nights within a wider trip. There is no equivalent of Canggu's month-long villa market.

**The drive** is roughly two to two and a half hours from the airport.

**Amenities** are limited. No international schools, basic medical, few restaurants outside the small resorts.

**Resale is very shallow.** The pool of buyers who want the east coast and can lawfully purchase is small.

## Volcanic risk

Mount Agung is active. The 2017 and 2018 eruptions triggered evacuations within the exclusion zone and repeatedly closed the airport, which stopped tourism to the whole island.

Check the exclusion zones for the specific location and how insurance treats it.

## Who it suits

Buyers wanting a home, a dive base or a small boutique operation, with a long horizon and no need for liquidity or income.

Not buyers modelling yield, and not anyone who may need to sell within five years.

## Common questions

### Is Amed a good place to buy in Bali?
For price, quiet and diving, yes. For rental yield and resale liquidity, it is among the weakest areas on the island.

### How far is Amed from the airport?
Roughly two to two and a half hours, longer in heavy traffic or rain.

### Is there rental demand in Amed?
Limited and seasonal, driven by diving and by travellers on a wider island circuit rather than long villa stays.

### Is it safe to buy near Mount Agung?
The volcano is active and has caused evacuations and airport closures. Risk concentrates on the slopes, so check exclusion zones and insurance treatment.

### How much is land in East Bali?
A fraction of the southern corridors, frequently below Tabanan. Zoning is the main constraint as most of it is agricultural.
