Almost everyone rents here before they buy, and the rental market tells you more about an area than any listing site will.

## What renting in Bali actually costs

**One-bedroom villas** start from around **IDR 17,500,000 a month in Canggu**, and around **IDR 15,600,000 in Seminyak**.

**Two-bedroom villas** run roughly **IDR 15 to 45 million a month**. That spread is not a mistake. Location, pool, condition, furnishing and how well you negotiate all move it substantially.

Within Canggu, **Berawa and Batu Bolong** carry a premium over nearly everywhere else. Central Ubud is not the bargain people expect, though prices drop quickly on the outskirts. **Sanur is consistently cheaper** than Canggu for equivalent space, which is one of the reasons families cluster there.

## The thing nobody warns you about

**Annual contracts are usually paid in full, up front.**

This is the single biggest gap between the budget people plan and the reality they meet. You do not pay monthly as you would at home. A landlord quoting an attractive annual rate commonly wants all twelve months in one transfer before handing over keys.

Monthly rentals exist and are far more flexible, but you pay a real premium for that flexibility. Most long-term listings want a **minimum of one to three months**, and many want six or twelve paid at once.

For anything beyond six months, an annual contract is normally better value if you can fund it.

## Monthly versus yearly

| | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rate per month | Higher | Lower |
| Payment | Rolling | Usually full amount up front |
| Flexibility | High | Low |
| Best for | First three months, testing an area | Anyone staying six months or more |

The sensible pattern is monthly for your first stretch while you work out where you actually want to be, then annual once you know.

## What to check before you sign

- **Is electricity included?** Usually not. Air conditioning across several bedrooms is a genuine monthly cost.
- **Who maintains the pool and garden?** If it is you, that is a staffing cost nobody quoted.
- **Is there a written contract?** A one-page note is common here and protects you very little.
- **What happens if you leave early?** On an annual prepayment, frequently nothing good. Get the exit terms in writing.
- **Water supply.** Ask where it comes from and whether it holds up in dry season.
- **Internet.** The villa's connection is not the area's connection. Test it before committing to anything long.
- **Flooding.** Ask what the street does in February. You will not see it during a dry-season viewing.

## Why this matters if you plan to buy

Rental prices are the closest thing Bali has to an honest signal about an area. If long-term rents are soft somewhere, the nightly rental market there is usually softer than the projections suggest.

More practically, renting for a year where you think you want to live is the cheapest due diligence available anywhere in this market. People are certain about their chosen area for roughly six months, and a lot of them then move.

**Rent first, buy second.** In that order it costs you a year of rent. In the other order it costs considerably more, because selling here is slow.

## Common questions

### How much is rent in Bali per month?
A one-bedroom villa starts around IDR 17.5 million a month in Canggu and IDR 15.6 million in Seminyak. Two-bedroom villas run roughly IDR 15 to 45 million depending on area and condition.

### Do you have to pay a year of rent up front in Bali?
Usually, yes, for the better annual rates. Most landlords offering a discounted yearly rate expect the full twelve months in a single payment before handover. Monthly rentals avoid this but cost meaningfully more per month.

### Is it cheaper to rent in Sanur or Canggu?
Sanur, consistently, for equivalent space. Canggu, and particularly Berawa and Batu Bolong, carries the highest premium on the island relative to what you get.

### What is the minimum rental period in Bali?
Most long-term listings want one to three months minimum. Many prefer six or twelve months, paid up front. Shorter stays are available but priced closer to holiday rates.

### Should I rent before buying in Bali?
Yes. A year of rent in your chosen area costs a fraction of what it costs to buy in the wrong one, and property here is slow to sell.
