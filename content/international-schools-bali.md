For most families, school fees are the single largest line in a Bali budget. Larger than rent, frequently by a wide margin.

## The range

Annual tuition runs from around **USD 3,000** at early-years bilingual centres to **over USD 30,000** at premium campuses.

The established international schools sit at roughly **USD 15,000 to 28,000 per student per year**, depending on grade level.

**Green School Bali**, in Ubud, runs approximately **USD 18,000 to 27,000+** a year depending on grade.

**Canggu Community School** runs from roughly **IDR 110 million** for Early Years to **IDR 257 million** for the IB Diploma years.

## The number nobody quotes you

Headline tuition is not what you pay. Once everything is added:

| | Annual |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | USD 500 to 2,500 |
| Uniforms | USD 150 to 400 |
| Transport | USD 1,200 to 3,600 |
| Lunch | USD 600 to 1,200 |

**All-in, budget USD 8,000 to 28,000 per child per year**, depending on school and grade.

For two children at an established school, that is a serious annual commitment before you have paid rent, and it is why family budgets here land in a completely different bracket to single or couple budgets.

## Where the schools are

**The Canggu and Pererenan corridor** serves the entrepreneurial and remote-working community. Practical, international, geared to families who arrived recently.

**Ubud** is home to the schools known internationally for creativity and sustainability, Green School most famously.

That geography matters more than people realise. **Pick the school first, then find housing near it.** Bali traffic turns a 12km school run into an hour each way, twice a day, and that decision shapes your entire life here far more than which beach you liked on holiday.

## What to check beyond the fee

- **Curriculum and where it leads.** IB, Cambridge and Australian systems are all present, and transferring between them mid-stream is disruptive.
- **Waiting lists.** Popular year groups fill. Places are not guaranteed because you can pay.
- **Fee escalation.** Ask what fees did over the last three years, not just what they are now.
- **What happens if you leave mid-year.** Refund policies vary considerably.
- **Whether the school sponsors your visa.** Some do, and it changes your immigration options.

## Before you buy anything

If you are relocating with children, the school decision comes before the property decision. Every time.

Rent near the school for a year first. If it works, buy nearby. If it does not, you have not tied a large amount of capital to the wrong side of the island.

### How much are international school fees in Bali?
Annual tuition runs from around USD 3,000 at early-years bilingual centres to over USD 30,000 at premium campuses. Established international schools sit at roughly USD 15,000 to 28,000 per student per year.

### What does Green School Bali cost?
Approximately USD 18,000 to 27,000 a year depending on grade level, before registration, transport, uniform and lunch.

Budget USD 8,000 to 28,000 per child per year all-in. Registration adds USD 500 to 2,500, uniforms USD 150 to 400, transport USD 1,200 to 3,600 and lunch USD 600 to 1,200.

### Where are the international schools in Bali?
Mainly the Canggu and Pererenan corridor, which serves the entrepreneurial and remote-working community, and Ubud, home to the schools known for creativity and sustainability.

### Should you choose the school or the area first?
The school, always. Bali traffic turns a 12km school run into an hour each way twice a day, so the school decides where you should live far more than the beach does.
