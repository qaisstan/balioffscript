Echo Beach — Pantai Batu Mejan — is where Canggu stops being about cafes and starts being about waves. It sits at the northern end of the strip, just before the Pererenan river mouth.

## What Echo Beach is

A black sand beach with a consistent reef-bottom wave that is more serious than Batu Bolong's, a row of beachfront restaurants and bars facing the break, and residential land running back inland.

The atmosphere is different from Batu Bolong a kilometre south. Less dense, less relentless, more surf-oriented. The crowd skews toward people who came for the wave rather than the coworking.

It is walkable in a limited sense — the beachfront strip itself is — but the surrounding residential areas are a scooter ride from most things.

## The market

Land here is cheaper than Batu Bolong and Berawa, and there is more of it, particularly as you move back from the beach and toward the river.

Stock is a mix of older properties on generous plots, newer villa developments, and some beachfront commercial. Plot sizes inland are better than central Canggu, which matters for delivering a proper pool within the KDB limit.

Lease terms at a given budget run longer than the Canggu core, frequently by five to ten years, which is the main commercial argument for the area.

## Rental performance

Good, with a surf-weighted guest profile.

Surf travellers book around swell rather than school holidays, which means Echo Beach fills some weeks that the rest of Canggu does not. Repeat rates are high and direct booking is more common than in the cafe-driven areas.

There is also solid family and group demand, drawn by larger properties and lower density than Berawa.

Nomad long-stay demand exists but is thinner than Batu Bolong's, because the working infrastructure is concentrated further south. The quiet months are correspondingly quieter.

Properties that perform are three and four bedrooms with proper pools and outdoor space, and anything with a genuine view of the break. Small units compete poorly against the volume of one and two bedroom stock further south.

## Against Batu Bolong and Berawa

**Echo Beach gives you** cheaper land, bigger plots, longer available terms, less traffic and a better wave.

**They give you** higher occupancy, stronger nomad demand filling the low season, more walkable amenity and a deeper resale pool.

At a mid-range budget, the longer term at Echo Beach usually wins on total return. At a high budget, the reliability of Berawa starts to justify itself.

## What to check here specifically

**Coastal setback**, on anything near the beach. The sempadan pantai restricts building within a defined distance of the high water line, and part of what looks like a beachfront parcel may not be buildable.

**River setback**, toward the Pererenan end. The sempadan sungai catches more parcels here than buyers expect and is strictly applied.

**Erosion and drainage.** This stretch of coast has seen erosion, and several inland streets flood in heavy rain. Ask about the specific parcel and street.

**Zoning for the exact parcel.** The beachfront commercial strip and the residential land behind it are zoned differently, and the boundary is not obvious.

**Access road width and surface**, which varies considerably on the inland lanes and affects both construction and guest arrival.

**Realistic comparables from Echo Beach itself**, not from Batu Bolong. The rate and occupancy profiles are genuinely different and using the wrong comparables will overstate your projection.

## Common questions

### Is Echo Beach cheaper than Batu Bolong?
Yes, meaningfully, with larger plots and lease terms typically five to ten years longer at the same budget.

### What kind of guest books at Echo Beach?
Surf travellers booking around swell, plus families and groups drawn by larger properties and lower density. Less nomad long-stay demand than Batu Bolong.

### Is Echo Beach walkable?
The beachfront strip is. The residential land behind it is a scooter ride from most amenity, unlike Batu Bolong where you can genuinely live without one.

### What should I check before buying at Echo Beach?
Coastal and river setbacks, erosion and flood history for the specific parcel, zoning at the commercial–residential boundary, and access road condition.

### Echo Beach or Pererenan?
Echo Beach for the wave and slightly more established amenity. Pererenan for cheaper land, longer terms and a rental market that has matured quickly.
