Seseh sits immediately north of Cemagi, at the point where the developed west coast corridor starts to thin out.

## What is there now

A black sand beach with a temple on it, a golf course, rice fields, and villas arriving at pace from the Pererenan direction.

It is roughly where Pererenan was in 2019: the infrastructure is behind the construction, and the character is changing faster than the roads are.

## The investment case

**Price.** Below Pererenan and well below Canggu, for coastline in the same corridor.

**Trajectory.** Development has moved north along this coast for twenty years. Seminyak, then Canggu, then Pererenan. Seseh is the next section of that line.

**Beach.** Uncrowded, dramatic, with strong currents in places.

## The risk

The same one Pererenan carries, one stage earlier. A large volume of near-identical villas is being built, and rental demand here is thinner than three kilometres south.

If you buy a generic three-bedroom pool villa in Seseh underwritten on Canggu occupancy, the numbers will not hold. Guests still concentrate where the cafes are.

Buying **land** here is a more defensible thesis than buying a rental business.

## What to verify

- Zoning on the parcel. Much of the surrounding land is agricultural
- Access road width, for construction and for guests
- Drainage and standing water in wet season
- Distance to Pererenan and Canggu at 6pm, not at 10am
- How many comparable villas are under construction within a kilometre

## Who it suits

Buyers with a five to ten year horizon who want coastline at a price the developed corridor no longer offers, and who do not need income in year one.

## Common questions

### Where is Seseh in Bali?
On the west coast immediately north of Cemagi and Pererenan, roughly 15 to 25 minutes from Canggu depending on traffic.

### Is Seseh a good investment?
As a land play with a long horizon, it has a credible case. As a nightly rental business, demand is thinner than the corridor to the south.

### How much is land in Seseh?
Below Pererenan and considerably below Canggu, with wide variation by zoning and access width.

### Is Seseh beach good?
Black sand, uncrowded and dramatic, with a temple on the shore. Currents can be strong, so it is not a casual swimming beach.

### Is Seseh the next Canggu?
Development has moved north along this coast for two decades, so the direction is credible. The timing is the risk, and nobody can price it precisely.
