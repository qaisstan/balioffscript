Indonesian property documents are in Indonesian, and the terms are not intuitive. These are the ones that decide things.

## Land titles

**Hak Milik (SHM)** Freehold. Perpetual. Indonesian citizens only. Never available to a foreigner.

**Hak Guna Bangunan (SHGB)** Right to build. Held by Indonesian legal entities including a PT PMA. Defined term, extendable.

**Hak Pakai (SHP)** Right of use. The only registered land right a foreigner can hold personally, requiring valid residency and meeting minimum value conditions.

**Hak Sewa** Lease. A contract, not a registered title. What most foreign buyers here actually hold.

**Hak Guna Usaha (HGU)** Right to cultivate, for agricultural enterprise.

**Girik / Petok** Not a certificate. Customary or tax evidence of possession on unregistered land. Trades at a discount for good reason.

## People and documents

**Notaris** Public notary who draws up deeds generally.

**PPAT** Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah. The official authorised specifically to make land deeds and lodge transfers. A transfer is not effective without one.

**BPN** The national land agency, where certificates are verified.

**AJB** Akta Jual Beli, the deed of sale and purchase executed before a PPAT.

## Building and zoning

**PBG** Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung. Building approval, required **before** construction. Replaced IMB.

**SLF** Sertifikat Laik Fungsi. Certifies the finished building is fit for use.

**KDB** Building coverage ratio. Maximum share of the plot the footprint may occupy.

**KLB** Floor area ratio. Total built floor area relative to plot size.

**KDH** Minimum green area that must stay unbuilt.

**Zona hijau** Green zone. Agricultural. Generally cannot host tourism accommodation.

## Company and licensing

**PT PMA** Foreign-owned limited liability company.

**NIB** Nomor Induk Berusaha. Business identification number issued through OSS.

**OSS** Online Single Submission. The licensing system.

**KBLI** The classification code for a business activity. Determines foreign ownership eligibility, capital thresholds and risk grading.

**NPWP** Taxpayer identification number.

## Tax and money

**BPHTB** Buyer's duty on acquisition of a titled right, around 5 percent.

**PBB** Annual land and building tax.

**THR** Mandatory religious holiday allowance for employees: one month's salary annually.

**BPJS** National health and employment social security. Employer contributions add roughly 14 percent.

## Measurement

**Are** 100 square metres. Land here is priced per are, not per square metre.

**Hektar** One hectare, 100 are, 10,000 square metres.

## Common questions

### What is SHM in Indonesia?
Sertifikat Hak Milik, the freehold certificate. Available only to Indonesian citizens.

### What is the difference between Hak Pakai and Hak Sewa?
Hak Pakai is a registered right of use in your own name at the land office. Hak Sewa is a lease contract with a landowner.

### What is PBG in Bali?
The building approval required before construction, which replaced the older IMB system.

### What is an are in Bali?
100 square metres. A 5 are plot is 500 square metres, and prices are quoted per are.

### What is BPHTB?
The buyer's acquisition duty on a titled transfer, around 5 percent of the transaction or assessed value.
