Indonesian property documents are in Indonesian, and the terms are not intuitive. These are the ones that decide things.

## Land titles

**Hak Milik (SHM)** Freehold. Perpetual. Indonesian citizens only. Never available to a foreigner.

**Hak Guna Bangunan (SHGB)** Right to build. Held by Indonesian legal entities including a PT PMA. Defined term, extendable.

**Hak Pakai (SHP)** Right of use. The only registered land right a foreigner can hold personally, requiring valid residency and meeting minimum value conditions.

**Hak Sewa** Lease. A contract, not a registered title, and **not recorded at BPN**. What most foreign buyers here actually hold.

**Hak Guna Usaha (HGU)** Right to cultivate, for agricultural enterprise.

**Hak Milik atas Satuan Rumah Susun** Strata title over an apartment unit. Available to foreigners above a minimum value.

**Girik / Petok** Not a certificate. Customary or tax evidence of possession on unregistered land. Trades at a discount for good reason.

**Pemecahan sertifikat** Splitting a certificate into smaller parcels. Requires minimum plot size and legal access for each resulting parcel.

## The two words that decide a lease

**Jaminan perpanjangan** Guaranteed extension. The landowner is contractually bound to grant it.

**Prioritas perpanjangan** Priority to extend — a right of first refusal at a price determined later. Worth far less, and what most leases actually contain.

Both translate into English as "extension rights". This is the most consequential distinction in a Bali lease.

## People and documents

**Notaris** Public notary who draws up deeds generally.

**PPAT** Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah. The official authorised specifically to make land deeds and lodge transfers, within a defined territory. A transfer is not effective without one.

**BPN** The national land agency, where certificates are verified.

**AJB** Akta Jual Beli, the deed of sale and purchase executed before a PPAT.

**Surat kuasa** Power of attorney. Cannot authorise what the law prohibits.

**Penerjemah tersumpah** Sworn translator. Required for documents carrying legal weight.

**Banjar / desa adat** The customary community organisation and village. Applies rules found in no statute, and the first forum for local disputes.

## Building and zoning

**PBG** Persetujuan Bangunan Gedung. Building approval, required **before** construction. Replaced IMB.

**SLF** Sertifikat Laik Fungsi. Certifies the finished building is fit for use. Without it a building is not lawfully occupiable.

**KDB** Building coverage ratio. Maximum share of the plot the footprint may occupy.

**KLB** Floor area ratio. Total built floor area relative to plot size.

**KDH** Minimum green area that must stay unbuilt.

**Sempadan pantai** Coastal setback from the high water line, within which building is restricted.

**Sempadan sungai** River setback. Catches more Canggu and Pererenan parcels than buyers expect.

**Zona hijau** Green zone. Agricultural. Generally cannot host tourism accommodation.

## Company and licensing

**PT PMA** Foreign-owned limited liability company.

**NIB** Nomor Induk Berusaha. Business identification number issued through OSS.

**OSS** Online Single Submission. The risk-based licensing system.

**KBLI** The classification code for a business activity. Determines foreign ownership eligibility, capital thresholds and risk grading. Current version is KBLI 2025 under BPS 7/2025.

**LKPM** Quarterly investment activity report required of PMA companies. Genuinely enforced.

**RUPS** Shareholder meeting, and the minutes recording it.

**Direksi / Komisaris** Management board and supervisory board of a company.

**RPTKA** The foreign worker utilisation plan a company must have approved before a foreign employee can be authorised.

**KPPA / KP3A** Representative office forms. May not generate revenue in Indonesia.

## Immigration

**KITAS** Limited stay permit. Several types, each authorising different activities.

**KITAP** Permanent stay permit, generally after continuous KITAS holding for a qualifying period.

**EPO** Exit Permit Only. Closes a stay permit when leaving permanently.

**SKTT** Certificate of residence for a KITAS holder, issued by the civil registry.

## Tax and money

**BPHTB** Buyer's duty on acquisition of a titled right, around 5 percent, on the higher of price and NJOP.

**NJOP** The government's assessed value of a parcel. The floor for BPHTB and a reference point in valuation.

**PPh final** Final tax. 2.5 percent of transfer value on a property disposal; 10 or 20 percent on rental income depending on residency.

**PBB** Annual land and building tax. Arrears attach to the land.

**NPWP** Taxpayer identification number.

**NITKU** Business unit identifier, issued per registered place of business.

**Coretax** The tax administration platform. Matches data strictly and rejects rather than queries.

**THR** Mandatory religious holiday allowance for employees: one month's salary annually.

**BPJS** National health and employment social security. Employer contributions are the larger share of the split.

**UMK** Regional minimum wage, set per regency and revised annually.

## Measurement

**Are** 100 square metres. Land here is priced per are, not per square metre.

**Hektar** One hectare, 100 are, 10,000 square metres.

## Common questions

### What is SHM in Indonesia?
Sertifikat Hak Milik, the freehold certificate. Available only to Indonesian citizens.

### What is the difference between Hak Pakai and Hak Sewa?
Hak Pakai is a registered right of use in your own name at the land office. Hak Sewa is a lease contract with a landowner, recorded nowhere.

### What is the difference between jaminan and prioritas perpanjangan?
Jaminan is a guaranteed extension the landowner is bound to grant. Prioritas is only a right of first refusal at a price set later, and is worth far less.

### What is PBG in Bali?
The building approval required before construction, which replaced the older IMB system. SLF certifies the finished building is fit for use.

### What is an are in Bali?
100 square metres. A 5 are plot is 500 square metres, and prices are quoted per are.

### What is BPHTB?
The buyer's acquisition duty on a titled transfer, around 5 percent, charged on whichever is higher of the transaction value and the NJOP assessed value.

### What is NJOP?
Nilai Jual Objek Pajak, the government's assessed value for a parcel, used as the floor for BPHTB and as a valuation reference point.
