These three end up on the same shortlist for people moving to Southeast Asia: remote workers, early retirees and families. They are very different places.

## Ownership

- **Bangkok and Chiang Mai:** you can own a **condo freehold** in your own name, within the building's **49% foreign quota**. Land cannot be owned; houses sit on a **registered lease of up to 30 years**.
- **Bali:** no ownership for foreigners. A **lease** (usually 25 to 30 years with an extension), **Hak Pakai** with residency, or **HGB through a PT PMA**.

If owning in your own name matters most, Thailand wins.

## What you get for the money

- **Bangkok:** high-rise condos, great transport, malls, hospitals, international schools. City life.
- **Chiang Mai:** lower costs, mountains, a slower pace, a large community of remote workers. Air quality is a real problem in the burning season, usually around March and April.
- **Bali:** villas with pools, beaches, surf, wellness. Traffic in the busy south, and more spread out.

## Renting it out

- **Bangkok** condos rent long term to locals and expats. Yields are steadier and lower, with little management.
- **Chiang Mai** rents to long-stay visitors; it is a smaller market.
- **Bali** villas earn from short-term holiday rentals, with higher yields (a well-run villa nets roughly **7 to 14%**) and more management work.

## Visas

Thailand's **Long-Term Resident** and **Thailand Privilege** visas are established. Indonesia has the **second home visa** and the **golden visa**, both newer. Both countries tax residents differently; check your home country's rules too.

## Which I would choose

- **City, own it outright, low effort:** a condo in Bangkok.
- **Cheap, calm base, long stays:** Chiang Mai, outside the smoke season.
- **Island life and a villa that pays:** Bali, with a long lease and a checked owner.

## Common questions

### Is Bali cheaper than Bangkok to live?
Day to day, they are similar for a comfortable life; housing type changes the budget most. A Bangkok condo usually costs less than a Bali villa with a pool.

### Can foreigners buy a house in Chiang Mai?
Not the land. A house sits on a registered lease of up to 30 years. Condos can be owned within the 49% quota.

### Which is better for digital nomads, Bali or Chiang Mai?
Both have big communities. Chiang Mai is cheaper and calmer; Bali has more going on, beaches and a stronger rental market if you buy.
