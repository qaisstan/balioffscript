Every answer you find online is somebody describing their own life and calling it a benchmark. A single person in a Canggu guesthouse and a family of four in Umalas are not living in the same country, financially.

So rather than one number, here is what actually moves it.

## The four things that decide your cost

**Where you live.** Canggu and Seminyak carry a premium that has nothing to do with quality. The same house in Sanur or Ubud costs meaningfully less. Moving twenty minutes changes your rent more than any other decision you will make.

**How you rent.** Yearly contracts, paid up front, are dramatically cheaper than monthly. That upfront lump is the single biggest gap between the budget people plan and the one they live.

**Whether you have children.** International school fees are the largest line item in any family budget here, by a wide margin. `Budget USD 8,000 to 28,000 per child per year all-in, once registration, transport, uniform and lunch are added to tuition`

**How much you live like a tourist.** Beach clubs, daily cafés and imported food will double a budget quietly. Locals-market cooking and warungs will halve it.

## What people underestimate

- **Visas.** Not free, and they recur. Budget for renewals and agent fees.
- **Healthcare.** Adequate locally for routine care, but serious cases are commonly flown to Singapore. Comprehensive international insurance is not optional.
- **Transport.** A scooter is cheap. A car and driver is not, and rainy season makes you want one.
- **Flights home.** Two or three a year adds up faster than any grocery bill.
- **The rainy season.** Roughly November to March. Damp, mould, repairs, and more money spent indoors.

## What it does not include

Buying property gives you no right to live here. Residence is a separate system with its own cost. The self-funded route is a deposit of around USD 130,000 held in an Indonesian bank, and [there are several other routes depending on what you will actually do here](/visas/).

Cross 183 days in a year and you become an Indonesian tax resident, whatever your visa says.

## The honest summary

Bali is cheaper than Australia, Europe or Singapore for the same standard of living. It is not cheap in the way it was ten years ago, and Canggu in particular has priced itself close to a mid-sized Western city for anything foreigner-facing.

Come for a long stay, including a rainy season, and track what you actually spend. That number is worth more than any article.
