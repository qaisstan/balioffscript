Most foreign-held property in Bali sits on Hak Sewa, the right to rent or lease. Understanding exactly what that is prevents most of the disappointment in this market.

## What it is, precisely

A **contractual right** to use land for an agreed period, granted by the landowner. It is not a registered land title in the way Hak Milik, HGB and Hak Pakai are.

That single fact drives everything:

- Your right is against **a person**, not recorded in the land register as ownership
- It depends on the landowner's title being sound
- It depends on the contract binding successors
- It cannot usually be used as security for lending

## Why it is still the most common route

It requires no residency, no company, no minimum value and no capital threshold. A foreigner can sign a lease on the same day they arrive.

For a home you will use for a defined period, well drafted, it is a legitimate and widely used instrument.

## What makes one lease good and another worthless

| Clause | What to demand |
| --- | --- |
| Term | Stated in years, with the start date defined |
| Extension | A fixed term and a **fixed price or formula**, not "market rate" |
| Notice | A defined window with a consequence for late notice that is not forfeiture |
| Successors | Binds heirs, successors and any future owner of the land |
| Building | States what happens to the structure at expiry |
| Assignment | You may transfer it, and on what conditions |
| Registration | Noted so a purchaser of the freehold discovers it |

"Extendable at market rate" is the clause that ruins leases. It reads as a right and functions as the landowner's option, priced at the moment your alternative is walking away from a building you paid for.

## The wasting asset problem

A lease declines in value as it runs down, and the decline accelerates near the end. A right with eight years left trades far below half the value of the same property with thirty, because your buyer inherits the expiry.

Model that from day one rather than discovering it when you sell.

## Before you sign

Have it reviewed by a notary you appointed. Confirm the person granting it actually owns the land, that any spouse or co-owner consents, and that there are no registered charges over the title.

An extension right granted by someone who does not own the land is worth nothing.

## Common questions

### What is Hak Sewa in Indonesia?
The right to lease or rent. It is a contractual right to use land for a defined period, not a registered ownership title.

### Is leasehold safe in Bali?
It is lawful and widely used. Its safety depends entirely on the drafting and on the landowner's title being sound.

### How long are Bali leaseholds?
Commonly 25 to 30 years, with extension terms that vary enormously in quality.

### Can you extend a lease in Bali?
Only on the terms your contract gives you. There is no automatic statutory right, which is why the extension clause matters more than the headline term.

### What is the difference between Hak Sewa and Hak Pakai?
Hak Sewa is a contract with a landowner. Hak Pakai is a registered right of use recorded in your own name, available to foreigners with valid residency.
