Moving house in Indonesia generates administrative work in several places, and skipping it produces a failure months later in a process you cared about.

## Where your address is recorded

**Your stay permit record** with immigration.

**Your SKTT** — the certificate of residence issued by the civil registry to a KITAS holder.

**Your tax registration**, the NPWP.

**Your bank**, which used the address for its own verification.

**Your company's registered address**, if you have one, in the deed, the NIB and the OSS record.

**Utility and service accounts.**

These are separate systems. Updating one does not update the others.

## Why it matters more than it sounds

Indonesian administration now runs through platforms that **match data strictly and reject rather than query**.

**Coretax**, the tax administration system, fails a filing where a name or address does not reconcile. It does not telephone you.

**Immigration** surfaces an inconsistency at a KITAS extension, which is exactly when you have no time to fix it.

**Banks** reject applications on mismatches rather than asking about them.

So an address updated with immigration but not with the civil registry, or updated in both but not on the tax record, is an inconsistency sitting there waiting for the moment you need something.

## When it bites

**At a KITAS extension**, where the address on the permit does not match the SKTT.

**At a tax filing**, where Coretax rejects on a data mismatch.

**At a bank**, opening an account or making a large transfer, where proof of address does not match the record.

**At a licence renewal**, if the company's registered address is stale.

**At a property transaction**, where identity documents have to reconcile.

**At a driving licence application**, which requires current proof of residence.

## What to update, in order

**The civil registry (Dukcapil)** for your **SKTT**, which is the document most other things rely on as proof of address.

**Immigration**, to reflect the change on your permit record. Reporting obligations on a change of address apply and the requirements vary by permit type.

**The tax office**, for your NPWP registration.

**Your bank.**

**Your company's records**, if applicable — the deed, the NIB and the OSS entry. A company whose OSS record does not match reality has a problem waiting, and any change to activity, address, shareholder or director has to be reflected.

**Utilities and services.**

## The documentation you need

**Evidence of the new address** — a tenancy agreement, a letter from the property owner, or confirmation from the local neighbourhood administration.

This catches people renting informally. A verbal arrangement with a landlord who will not provide written confirmation makes the whole chain difficult, which is a practical reason to have a proper written tenancy even for a modest rental.

## The name problem, which travels with this

While you are reconciling records, check the **name spelling matches your passport exactly** — including middle names and their order — across every document.

Indonesian systems reject on variations a person would read straight past. A passport renewed since an earlier registration is a common source of two records that do not reconcile, and it is much easier to fix before you need something than during.

## For property owners who leave

If you move abroad and still own property here, your records need attention rather than abandonment.

**Someone has to be able to act** — a properly scoped power of attorney drafted by your own notary, specific, time-limited and with defined limits, particularly on receiving funds.

**Your tax position changes.** Ceasing Indonesian tax residence generally moves rental income from ten percent final to twenty percent non-resident.

**Keep the bank account alive** if you need to receive rental income and pay PBB, utilities and staff, and keep the **phone number** alive too, because it is frequently tied to banking two-factor authentication.

## Common questions

### Do I need to report an address change in Indonesia?
Yes. Reporting obligations apply on a change of address, with requirements varying by permit type, and the SKTT and immigration records both need updating.

### What happens if my address is not updated everywhere?
Inconsistencies surface as rejections — at a KITAS extension, a tax filing, a bank application or a licence renewal — because systems match strictly rather than querying.

### Which record should I update first?
The civil registry for your SKTT, because it is the document most other processes rely on as proof of address, then immigration, the tax office and your bank.

### What proof of address do I need in Indonesia?
A tenancy agreement, a letter from the property owner or confirmation from the local neighbourhood administration, which is why a written tenancy matters even for a modest rental.

### Does my name spelling matter on Indonesian records?
Substantially. Systems reject on variations a person would read past, and a passport renewed since an earlier registration commonly leaves two records that do not reconcile.
