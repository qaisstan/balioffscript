This is the first question almost every buyer asks, and it contains a false premise.

## Freehold is not on the menu

**Hak Milik**, Indonesian freehold, may only be held by Indonesian citizens. Not by foreigners with residency, not through a marriage, not through any structure.

So leasehold versus freehold is not a choice you get to make. Anything marketed to a foreigner as freehold is one of three things: a lease described inaccurately, a company holding HGB, or a nominee arrangement that gives you nothing enforceable.

## What the real comparison is

| | Leasehold | Hak Pakai | HGB via PT PMA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nature | Contract with the landowner | Registered right of use | Registered building right |
| In whose name | Yours, contractually | Yours, at the land office | Your company's |
| Residency needed | No | Yes, KITAS or KITAP | No, but you run a company |
| Minimum value | No | Yes, houses and apartments | Capital thresholds apply |
| Term | Whatever the contract says | Defined, extendable | Defined, extendable |
| Use | Anything the lease permits | Residence | Business, subject to classification |
| Ongoing cost | None | None | Annual compliance and filings |
| Financeable | Rarely | Occasionally | More possible, still hard |
| On your death | Only if drafted to bind heirs | Registered, heirs face disposal rules | Passes as shares |

## How to choose

**A home, and you hold a KITAS.** Hak Pakai. The only route putting a registered right in your own name at the land office.

**A home, no residency, smaller budget.** A well-drafted long leasehold. The drafting is the entire product.

**A business or a development.** HGB through a PT PMA, provided the classification you need is open. Bali closed villa and homestay to new foreign-owned registration on 22 July 2026.

**Anything described as freehold for foreigners.** Walk.

## The clause that decides a leasehold's value

"Extendable at market rate" is not an extension right. It is the landowner's option, priced at the moment your alternative is abandoning a building you paid for.

Demand a stated extension term, a fixed price or a formula tied to a published index, a defined notice window, and language binding heirs and successors.

## Why people still choose leasehold

It needs no residency, no company, no capital threshold and no minimum value. It is the fastest lawful route into the market, and for a defined-period home it is a legitimate instrument used by serious buyers.

It is also a wasting asset, and it should be priced as one. A right with eight years left trades far below half the value of the same property with thirty.

## Common questions

### Can foreigners buy freehold in Bali?
No. Hak Milik is reserved for Indonesian citizens and no structure changes that. Anything sold to a foreigner as freehold is something else.

### Is leasehold or freehold better in Bali?
The choice is not available to you. Compare leasehold against Hak Pakai and HGB through a company, which are the three lawful foreign routes.

### How long is leasehold in Bali?
Commonly 25 to 30 years. The extension clause matters considerably more than the headline term.

### What is the safest ownership route for a foreigner in Bali?
Hak Pakai if you hold residency, because it is a registered right in your own name. Otherwise a properly drafted long lease.

### Does a PT PMA give me freehold?
No. A PT PMA holds HGB, a registered building right with a defined term. It is not Hak Milik.
