A small boutique hotel in Bali is a dream for many investors. It is also where the rules are strictest, because the province is trying to slow down new accommodation in parts of the island.

## Hotel, villa or guesthouse: the code matters

Indonesian law classifies accommodation by **business code (KBLI)**: starred hotels, non-starred hotels, villas and other short stays, guesthouses (pondok wisata), apartment hotels. Each has its own rules on size, classification and who may own the company.

- **Starred hotels** need a formal star classification. Running an unclassified hotel under a hotel code risks losing the tourism licence.
- **Guesthouses (pondok wisata)** are reserved for Indonesian owners.
- In 2026 Bali **restricted new PT PMA registrations** in a number of business codes and asked for closures of low-risk codes to foreign companies. Which accommodation codes are open to foreign companies in Bali has changed during the year. Confirm the exact code in OSS, with a notary, before anything else.

## The land and the zone

A hotel needs a plot in a **tourism-zoned** area, with building limits (KDB/KLB) that allow the rooms you plan, under Bali's **15-metre height limit**. Building on **agricultural land** is banned. In **Tabanan, Jembrana, Buleleng, Bangli, Karangasem and Klungkung**, new accommodation permits have been restricted since the 2025 floods.

## The company and the money

You need a **PT PMA** with the hotel code, **IDR 2.5 billion** paid-up capital and an investment plan above **IDR 10 billion per code per location**, excluding land and buildings. A real hotel costs much more: land or lease, design, construction, fit-out, licensing and working capital until it fills.

## Permits

**PBG** before building, **SLF** before opening, the **tourism business licence**, star classification for starred hotels, environmental documents depending on size, and registration for the local hotel tax.

## Is a hotel the right product?

For most private investors, **a small villa complex or a few villas** is simpler to permit, cheaper to run and easier to sell. A hotel makes sense with hospitality experience, an operator, and money that can wait for the business to grow.

## Common questions

### Can a foreigner own a hotel in Bali?
Through a PT PMA with an accommodation code open to foreign companies, on land held on HGB or a lease. Which codes are open in Bali changed in 2026, so check first.

### Can I build a hotel anywhere in Bali?
No. Only in zones that allow tourism accommodation, never on agricultural land, and with restrictions in six regencies since 2025.

### What is the difference between a hotel and a villa licence?
They are different business codes with different rules on classification, size and foreign ownership. Choose the code first, then the land.

### How long does it take to open a hotel in Bali?
Usually years, from land checks and permits through construction and classification to opening.
