Bali does honeymoons better than almost anywhere, partly because a private villa with a pool costs what a hotel room costs in Europe. The mistakes are predictable: too many bases, the wrong area, and booking on a photograph.

## The two-base structure

**Nights 1 to 4: the hills.** Ubud, Sidemen or Munduk. Cool at night, green, quiet, and where the spa-and-rice-field version of the trip lives.

**Nights 5 to 9: the coast.** Uluwatu for the cliffs, Seminyak for the restaurants and beach clubs, Sanur or Amed for calm water, Nusa Dua if you want a resort to do the thinking.

Two bases, one move, half a day in the car. Three or four bases is how people spend a honeymoon being driven around.

## The areas, plainly

**Ubud.** Jungle valleys, river gorges, yoga, spas, the best restaurants outside the south, and rain. The classic honeymoon photograph with a pool hanging over a gorge is here. It is 90 minutes from a beach.

**Uluwatu and the Bukit.** The most dramatic scenery in Bali: white cliffs, limestone, turquoise water. Spectacular clifftop hotels and villas. The beaches are down staircases and the drives between things are longer than they look.

**Seminyak and Petitenget.** The best dinner scene on the island, walkable, good hotels, a dark-sand beach with sunsets and strong currents.

**Sanur.** Calm, shallow, protected water, sunrise instead of sunset, quieter and older. Underrated for couples who want to swim rather than photograph.

**Sidemen.** Rice terraces under Agung, almost no tourism, a handful of beautiful small properties. The place people fall in love with and cannot explain afterwards.

**Amed and Menjangan.** For a diving honeymoon, black sand, calm sea and no crowds.

**Nusa Lembongan.** A short boat from Sanur, with proper island pace and very good small resorts.

## What it costs

- **Boutique hotel, good area:** 120 to 250 USD a night.
- **Private one-bedroom pool villa:** 150 to 350.
- **Clifftop suite with the view you have seen online:** 400 to 1,200.
- **A good dinner for two with wine:** 80 to 160, and wine is the expensive part because of import duty.
- **Couples spa treatment, two hours:** 60 to 150 at a good independent spa, three times that in a resort.

Prices drop 30 to 50 per cent in February, and peak from July to August and over Christmas.

## Booking honestly

**Check the pool photograph against the map.** A wide-angle lens and a well-chosen crop hide the neighbouring construction site and the road. Look at recent guest photographs rather than the listing gallery, and check the satellite view for building work next door.

**Read the reviews for noise and access.** Uluwatu and Canggu both have active construction. A villa on a narrow lane may be 200 metres from the road and delivered to on foot.

**Ask what is within walking distance.** Nothing at all is normal in many villa areas, which is fine if you plan for a driver and unpleasant if you do not.

**Check the season for view.** A clifftop room in February may be facing grey water for three days.

## Things worth doing

Sunset at Uluwatu temple with the kecak performance. A private boat to Nusa Penida or the Gilis for a day. Sunrise on Batur if you can face 3am. A cooking class. Dinner on the sand at Jimbaran, which is touristy and lovely anyway. A proper spa day, which here costs what a massage costs at home.

The one to skip if time is short: a full-day temple tour that covers four sites. Two is plenty.

## Common questions

### How many days do you need in Bali for a honeymoon?
Ten to twelve, split between two bases. Anything shorter and one base is better than two.

### What is the most romantic area of Bali?
Ubud and Sidemen for green and quiet, Uluwatu for drama, Sanur and Amed for calm water. Most couples want one of each.

### How much does a Bali honeymoon cost?
Excluding flights, roughly 250 to 500 USD a day for a good version, more for clifftop suites, and substantially less in February.

### Is Bali good for a honeymoon in rainy season?
Yes, at a discount, if you stay in the south and accept afternoon rain. The highlands are wetter and cloudier.

### Do Bali hotels do honeymoon extras?
Almost all of them, and they are generous with it. Tell them at booking rather than on arrival.
