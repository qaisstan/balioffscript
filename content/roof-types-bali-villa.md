The roof is the single largest maintenance item in a Bali villa and the one most likely to be specified for how it looks rather than how it performs.

## Alang-alang thatch

The classic Balinese roof, and the one in the photographs.

**Why people choose it.** It is genuinely beautiful, it photographs extremely well, it is cool because it insulates and breathes, and it is what guests picture when they imagine Bali.

**The cost.** Modest to install relative to alternatives.

**The problem.** It has a **finite life measured in years, not decades**. Depending on quality, thickness, treatment and exposure, expect to be re-thatching within a single-digit number of years, and sooner on an exposed or coastal building.

Re-thatching is a significant job. It is expensive, it is disruptive, and it takes a rental property out of service while it happens. Owners who did not budget for it are surprised twice — by the cost and by the lost season.

**It also burns**, it houses insects, and it can leak where it has thinned.

**If you use it:** specify good quality material, proper thickness, and treatment. Budget re-thatching as a scheduled capital item rather than an emergency, and schedule it in the low season.

## Shingle

**Wooden shingle**, frequently ulin or similar durable timber.

Longer-lived than alang-alang, still natural in appearance, and considerably more expensive to install.

Good compromise for owners who want the aesthetic without the replacement cycle, and it ages well.

## Tile

**Clay or concrete tile**, which is what most Indonesian buildings actually use.

**Long-lived**, low maintenance, weatherproof and inexpensive relative to its life.

Less distinctive in photographs, though a well-designed tiled roof with good proportions and overhangs does not read as cheap. Plenty of excellent Bali villas are tiled.

For a property where operating cost matters more than the roof appearing in the cover image, this is usually the right answer.

## Metal

Used for outbuildings, carports and utility structures.

Cheap, durable, and noisy in rain — which in Bali matters, because the rain is not gentle and a metal roof over a bedroom is a guest complaint.

## The hybrid, which is what most people should do

**Tile or shingle over the enclosed, air-conditioned, serviced parts** — bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen. Long-lived, low maintenance, weatherproof, quiet.

**Alang-alang over the open living pavilion**, where it is spectacular, appropriate and where the replacement cycle is on a smaller area that is easier to work on.

This gets you the photograph and the atmosphere without carrying a short replacement cycle across the whole building. Most well-run Bali villas are built this way.

## What matters more than the material

**Overhangs.** Deep eaves keep rain off walls and windows, shade the building and reduce the cooling load. This single design decision affects maintenance, comfort and electricity more than the roof material does.

**Insulation.** Frequently omitted in Bali builds and it matters enormously. An uninsulated roof turns the building into a heat store, which your aircon then fights all night.

**Drainage.** Guttering, downpipes and where the water actually goes. Bali rainfall finds every weakness, and roof drainage failure is the most expensive defect to fix after the fact because of what it damages on the way.

**Ventilation** in the roof space, which prevents heat build-up and mould.

## When buying an existing villa

**Ask what the roof is and when it was last done.** An unknown answer on alang-alang means it is due, and that is a quantifiable reason to reduce an offer.

**Look at every ceiling** for staining, which is the evidence of a leak whether or not it is leaking today.

**Check the guttering and downpipes**, and where the water discharges.

**Inspect the roof space** if accessible, for timber condition, termite evidence and insulation.

Get a quote for anything that needs doing and put it on the table as an itemised deduction.

## Common questions

### How long does an alang-alang roof last in Bali?
A finite life measured in years rather than decades, depending on material quality, thickness, treatment and exposure, with coastal and exposed buildings needing it sooner.

### What is the best roof for a Bali villa?
A hybrid in most cases — tile or shingle over enclosed air-conditioned rooms for durability, and alang-alang over an open living pavilion for the appearance.

### Is a thatched roof expensive to maintain?
Installation is modest but replacement is a significant scheduled cost that takes a rental property out of service, and it should be budgeted as a capital item rather than an emergency.

### Does roof choice affect cooling costs?
Substantially, through insulation and overhangs. An uninsulated roof turns the building into a heat store that aircon then fights all night.

### What should I check about a roof before buying a villa?
What material it is and when it was last done, every ceiling for staining, guttering and where water discharges, and the roof space for timber condition and insulation.
