Knowing how long this actually takes removes the seller's main weapon, which is urgency.

## The realistic schedule

**Days 1 to 3.** Appoint your own notary and legal reviewer. Obtain copies of the certificate, the seller's identity documents, and evidence of authority to sell.

**Days 3 to 7.** Certificate verified at the land office. Registered holder confirmed. Registered charges checked. Title type and remaining term confirmed against the record rather than the photocopy.

**Days 5 to 10.** Zoning checked on the specific parcel through the spatial planning system. Buildable envelope established from KDB, KLB, green area and setbacks.

**Days 7 to 12.** Boundaries walked against the certificate. Access route inspected and the right of way confirmed as registered rather than customary. Road width checked for construction access.

**Days 10 to 14.** For built property: PBG and SLF obtained and compared against the building as it stands, extensions included. Operating licence checked on OSS, including whether codes migrated to KBLI 2025 and whether it transfers.

**Days 12 to 21.** Spouse and heir consents. Tax receipts. Findings written up. Only now does a deposit make sense.

## What extends it

Inheritance in the title history. Unregistered or girik land. Disputed boundaries. Zoning questions. Multiple owners. A seller acting under power of attorney.

Each of these is a reason to slow down, not speed up.

## What should never compress it

Another buyer looking. A price rising Monday. An owner who needs to close this week.

Land listed for eight months does not become urgent because you walked onto it. If a seller will not allow two to three weeks for standard verification, the verification is the reason.

## The written output

Insist your notary puts the findings in writing: certificate authenticity, registered holder, title type, remaining term, encumbrances, zoning, boundaries, permits.

If it is not written down, it was not checked.

## Common questions

### How long does it take to buy property in Bali?
A clean titled transfer moves in weeks. Verification alone is one to three weeks, and registration afterwards takes further weeks.

### Can due diligence be done faster?
It can be rushed. Everything that goes wrong in this market goes wrong because it was.

### What if the seller will not wait?
Treat that as the finding. A genuine seller with clean documents does not lose a deal over a fortnight.

### What takes longest to verify?
Anything involving inheritance, unregistered land, or a building whose permits do not match what is standing.

### When should I pay a deposit?
After verification is complete, against written refund conditions tied to specific named checks.
