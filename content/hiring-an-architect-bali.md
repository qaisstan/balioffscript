The most expensive decision in a Bali build is made on paper, months before anything is poured, and it is routinely handed to whoever the contractor recommends.

## Why the design matters more here

In a market where the villa competes on photographs, the design is the product. Two villas on adjacent plots with identical build costs can achieve nightly rates twenty or thirty percent apart, entirely because one was designed to photograph and live well and the other was drawn to fit rooms onto a plot.

The design also determines whether you clear the permit. A PBG application requires proper technical drawings and structural calculations assessed against the zone's KDB, KLB, height limit and setbacks. A drawing that ignores any of those is a rejected application and months lost.

And it determines cost. The difference between an efficient structural grid and an inefficient one is commonly ten to fifteen percent of the build, which dwarfs the entire design fee.

## What it costs

Architectural fees in Bali typically run **five to ten percent of construction cost** for a full service — concept, developed design, technical drawings, permit documentation and construction supervision.

Cheaper arrangements exist. A drafter producing permit drawings from a layout you specify costs a fraction of that, and for a simple, conventional building it can be adequate. What you lose is the design thinking, the material knowledge and the supervision.

On a USD 300,000 build, a full architectural service at seven percent is USD 21,000. If it improves the nightly rate by fifteen percent on USD 60,000 of annual revenue, it repays itself in under three years and keeps paying for the rest of the term.

## What good looks like in Bali specifically

**Climate.** Cross-ventilation, deep overhangs, orientation away from the worst of the afternoon sun, and drainage that handles a tropical downpour rather than an average rainfall figure. Bali buildings fail on water and heat more than anything else.

**Materials that survive humidity.** Timber selection, the right steel treatment, and an understanding of what mould does to what. An architect who has only worked in temperate climates will specify things that fail here within two years.

**The tri mandala ordering.** The spatial relationship between sacred, living and service areas relative to mountain and sea. Good architects working in Bali design with it, and buildings that ignore it sit badly on their sites.

**Indoor-outdoor.** The single biggest driver of what a Bali villa achieves per night. The pool's relationship to the living space, the garden's relationship to the bedrooms, and where the light lands at the times guests photograph.

**Buildability with local trades.** A detail that requires a technique local contractors do not use will be executed badly or not at all. An architect who knows the trades designs to what they do well.

## How to choose one

**Visit completed buildings**, not renders. Two or three, ideally a few years old, so you can see how the materials have aged. This is the whole assessment.

**Ask who supervises construction.** Design without supervision is a drawing set the contractor interprets. Site supervision is where the design survives or does not.

**Check their permit record.** Have their projects obtained PBG and SLF without difficulty? That is a practical measure of whether the work is technically sound.

**Understand the fee structure.** What is included, at what stage payments fall due, how many revisions, whether supervision is in scope and how often they visit.

**Get the deliverables in writing.** Concept drawings, developed design, technical drawings, structural calculations, permit documentation, material schedules, supervision visits. Ambiguity here is where relationships break.

## The independence question

An architect recommended by your contractor works for a relationship with the contractor, not for you. That is not necessarily bad, and it is a conflict you should see clearly.

The value of an independent architect is that someone technically competent checks the contractor's work against the drawings and against you. On a project where you are not present daily and are funding in stages, that is worth a great deal.

## Common questions

### How much does an architect cost in Bali?
Typically five to ten percent of construction cost for a full service including permit documentation and construction supervision. Drafting-only arrangements cost far less and deliver far less.

### Do I need an architect to get a PBG?
The application requires proper technical drawings and structural calculations. A qualified professional has to produce them, whether an architect or an engineer working with a drafter.

### Should I use the contractor's architect?
It is a conflict worth seeing clearly. An independent architect supervising construction checks the contractor's work against the drawings on your behalf, which matters when you are funding in stages and not present daily.

### Does good design increase villa rental income in Bali?
Substantially. Two villas with identical build costs can achieve nightly rates twenty or thirty percent apart based on design, because the photographs are what sell the booking.

### What do Bali architects most often get wrong?
Water and heat. Drainage designed for average rainfall rather than a tropical downpour, and insufficient shading and cross-ventilation, are the two failures that cost owners most after handover.
