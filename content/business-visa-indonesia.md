The business visa sits between tourism and employment, and the boundary is narrower than most people assume.

## What it covers

Meetings, negotiations, contract discussions, conferences, exhibitions, site inspections, supplier visits and due diligence. Activities where you are representing a foreign company and not performing work inside Indonesia.

For a foreign buyer visiting notaries, inspecting land and meeting contractors, either a visitor visa or a business visa is generally appropriate depending on the specifics.

## What it does not cover

Performing work. Delivering services to an Indonesian client. Managing local staff day to day. Taking up a role in an Indonesian company. Earning income sourced in Indonesia.

The distinction is between **discussing** business and **doing** it. Attending a meeting about a construction project is covered; supervising the site as its project manager is not.

## Single and multiple entry

Single entry suits a one-off trip. Multiple entry, valid over a longer period with a maximum stay per visit, suits anyone travelling here repeatedly.

For a buyer running a project remotely with several visits a year, multiple entry is usually the practical choice.

## The enforcement context

Since the Dharma Dewata task force became operational on 15 April 2026, activity rather than payment has been the test applied. Roughly 100 officers across Canggu, Ubud, Seminyak, Kerobokan and Uluwatu detained 62 foreign nationals in the first three weeks.

A business visa is not a workaround for working here. Someone using one to run an Indonesian operation day to day is exposed in exactly the same way as someone working on a tourist visa.

## When you need something else

If you are taking a role in a PT PMA, a work KITAS or investor KITAS is the correct instrument. If you are employed by an Indonesian entity, a work permit is required.

If you are working remotely for a foreign employer while here, the E33G is the route, not a business visa.

## Sponsorship

Most business visa categories require an Indonesian sponsor: a company inviting you, or an agent acting in that capacity. The sponsor carries responsibility for your compliance while you are here.

If you already hold a PT PMA, the company can generally sponsor your business visits, which is simpler than arranging it each time through an agent.

## How long you can stay

Duration and extension rules differ by category, and multiple-entry variants cap the length of each individual visit rather than the total period of validity.

Track both numbers. Holding a visa valid for a year does not mean you may remain for a year, and people conflate the two and overstay without intending to.

## Keep evidence of purpose

Carry something showing why you are here: meeting confirmations, correspondence with an Indonesian counterparty, contracts under negotiation, or documents relating to the property you are buying.

Given the current enforcement posture, being able to demonstrate that your activity matches your permit is worth the small effort of keeping it to hand.

## Common questions

### Can I attend business meetings in Indonesia on a tourist visa?
Meetings are generally acceptable, but a business visa is the cleaner instrument where the purpose of travel is commercial. Neither permits work.

### What is the difference between a business visa and a work permit in Indonesia?
A business visa covers meetings and negotiations for a foreign company. A work permit authorises performing work in Indonesia, usually with a sponsoring employer.

### Can I run my Bali business on a business visa?
No. Managing an Indonesian operation day to day is work and requires the appropriate KITAS and authorisation.

### Is a multiple entry business visa worth it?
For anyone visiting several times a year for a project or investment, yes. It avoids reapplying for each trip.

### Can I do property due diligence on a business visa?
Yes. Viewing property, meeting notaries and inspecting sites are covered, and buying as a purchaser is not employment.
