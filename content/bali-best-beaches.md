Bali's beaches vary more than almost any island this size: white limestone sand in the south, black volcanic sand on the west and north, reef-protected lagoons on the east, and open Indian Ocean swell on the west.

## For clear turquoise water and white sand

**Melasti**, Ungasan. White sand at the bottom of a spectacular limestone-cut road, with road access rather than stairs. The most photogenic accessible beach on the mainland.

**Padang Padang**, Uluwatu. Small, reached through a cleft in the rock, famous and crowded by midday.

**Balangan**. A long stretch under the cliff with warungs on the sand and a good surf break at the end.

**Bingin**. Narrow, dramatic, with warungs built into the cliff and a serious reef wave in front.

**Nyang Nyang**. Enormous, empty, and a long steep descent that keeps it that way.

**Green Bowl**. A cave, a small beach and a long staircase.

For all of the Bukit beaches: check the tide. Several shrink to nothing at high tide and expose sharp reef at low.

## For calm water and swimming

**Sanur**, reef-protected, shallow, flat, with a beach path. The best family beach in Bali.

**Nusa Dua**, the calmest water in the south, clean and patrolled.

**Jimbaran**, a wide sheltered bay with seafood on the sand at sunset.

**Amed and Tulamben**, black pebble, calm and the best shore snorkelling on the mainland.

**Lovina and Pemuteran**, in the north, facing the Java Sea with no swell at all.

## For surfing

**Kuta and Legian** for learning: sand bottom, long beach break, lifeguards, schools.

**Batu Bolong and Echo Beach**, Canggu, for beginners and intermediates.

**Uluwatu, Padang Padang, Impossibles, Bingin**, for experienced surfers on reef.

**Medewi**, west Bali, a long left-hander and a quiet village.

**Keramas, Sanur, Serangan, Nusa Dua** in wet season, when the east coast turns on.

## For sunset

**Seminyak and Petitenget**, the classic beanbag-and-beer sunset.

**Echo Beach**, Canggu, with bars along the sand.

**Tanah Lot**, for the temple silhouette, with the crowd that comes with it.

**Single Fin at Uluwatu**, which is a cliff rather than a beach and better than all of them.

## For empty sand

**Nyang Nyang**, Bukit. **Pasut and Kelating**, Tabanan, black sand and nobody. **Yeh Gangga** and **Kedungu**, west of Canggu. **Amed's smaller coves**. **Pantai Lima and Seseh**, between Canggu and Tanah Lot. **The far west toward Medewi and Gilimanuk**.

## The black sand question

Half of Bali's beaches are volcanic and the sand is grey to black. It is not dirty; it is basalt. It gets very hot underfoot in the middle of the day, and it photographs darker than people expect from the brochures. Canggu, Seminyak, Medewi, Lovina and Amed are all dark-sand coasts. The white sand is concentrated on the Bukit and the eastern islands.

## Safety, which matters more here than the list

The west coast has powerful rip currents and most beaches outside Kuta, Legian, Seminyak and Nusa Dua are unpatrolled. Read [rip currents and beach safety](/travel/bali-beach-safety/) before swimming anywhere on the west or south coast. On the Bukit, watch the tide and wear reef shoes.

## Beach clubs and the day-bed economy

Most of the famous beach names in the south now come with a beach club: a day bed, a minimum spend and a pool. It is a pleasant way to spend an afternoon and it is not the beach. The free version is 50 metres away with a sarong and a warung.

## Common questions

### Which beach in Bali has the clearest water?
The Bukit beaches — Melasti, Padang Padang, Balangan — and the east coast around Amed. The Nusa Islands are clearer still.

### Are Bali beaches white sand?
Some are. The Bukit peninsula has white limestone sand; the west and north coasts are black volcanic sand.

### Which Bali beach is best for families?
Sanur for calm shallow water and a flat beach path, Nusa Dua for facilities, Jimbaran for a sheltered bay.

### Why is Bali beach sand black?
It is volcanic basalt, ground down over time. It is clean, and it gets hot in the sun.

### Are Bali beaches crowded?
The famous ones in the south are, from mid-morning. Tabanan, the far west, the north and the smaller Bukit coves are quiet even in August.
