Building here is genuinely rewarding and consistently slower than people plan for. Not because of construction. Because of everything before it.

## The order

**Spatial confirmation first.** Before design, before anything, confirm what the parcel permits. This is the gate. Everything downstream depends on it and it is the step people skip because it feels administrative.

**Then design to the constraints,** not to the view. Building coefficients cap your footprint and your total floor area. Setbacks come off before either applies. A beautiful design that ignores them gets redrawn.

**Then the building approval.** Submitted through the national system, verified administratively, then technically.

**Then build.** Then the certificate of functionality, which is what makes the finished building legally usable.

## Realistic timelines, from empty land to approval

| Regency | Time to approval |
| --- | --- |
| Denpasar | 3 to 5 months |
| Gianyar | 4 to 5 months |
| Tabanan | 4 to 5 months |
| Badung | 5 to 6 months |
| Klungkung | 5 to 6 months |

Badung is where most people build and the slowest to approve, because volume creates the queue.

Add construction on top of that. Then the functionality certificate after.

## What people do not budget for

**Time.** Six months of approvals before a shovel moves is normal, not a delay.

**Resubmission.** Most of the delay in those figures is documents going back for deficiencies, not government processing. Complete files move.

**The environmental document,** at the level your project size requires.

**Neighbour consent,** which is a real step and occasionally a real negotiation.

**Site access for materials.** A plot down a narrow lane costs more to build on than the same plot on a road, and nobody prices this at purchase.

## The mistake that costs the most

Buying the land before confirming what it permits. It is the single most expensive error in this market, and it is entirely avoidable in about two weeks of checking.

## Common questions

### How much does it cost to build a villa in Bali?
Construction is quoted per square metre and the range between basic and high specification is wide. Get the quote broken into structure, finishes, pool, landscaping and furniture, because bundled figures hide where the money goes.

### How long does it take to build a villa in Bali?
A straightforward villa typically runs the better part of a year once permits are in hand. Permits themselves, wet season and material delays are what push projects past a year.

### Do you need a permit to build in Bali?
Yes. PBG is the building approval and SLF certifies the completed building is fit for use. Starting without PBG is how projects end up demolished or unable to be licensed.

### What are the biggest risks when building in Bali?
Building on land whose zoning does not permit it, paying ahead of progress, and no independent supervision. All three are avoidable and all three are common.

### Should you use a fixed-price or cost-plus contract?
Fixed price transfers risk to the builder but invites specification disputes. Whichever you choose, tie payments to inspected milestones rather than to dates.
