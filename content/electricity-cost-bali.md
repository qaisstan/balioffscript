Electricity is the running cost that surprises people most, because the baseline they budget from is a European or Australian home that does not run four air conditioners against tropical humidity.

## How supply works

PLN is the national utility. Connections are sold by capacity, measured in VA, and the capacity you hold determines both your standing charge and what you can run simultaneously.

| Capacity | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| 1,300 to 2,200 VA | Small house, one or two air conditioners |
| 3,500 to 5,500 VA | Three-bedroom villa with pool |
| 7,700 VA and above | Larger villa, multiple units, heavy pool plant |

Undersized capacity is a common problem in older properties. If the main breaker trips whenever the kitchen and two air conditioners run together, the answer is an upgrade, and upgrades cost money and take time.

## Prepaid versus postpaid

Prepaid meters are topped up like phone credit and are common in rentals. They make budgeting visible and prevent bill shock, but running out at 2am with guests in the villa is its own problem.

Postpaid is billed monthly. For an operating villa it is generally the better arrangement, with someone responsible for paying it on time.

## What drives the bill

**Air conditioning**, overwhelmingly. Each unit running continuously is the single largest draw, and guests run them continuously with the doors open.

**Pool pump.** Several hours daily, every day, whether or not anyone swims.

**Water heating**, particularly where there are multiple bathrooms.

**Water pumps**, on properties with tanks and pressure systems.

A three-bedroom villa in full occupancy with air conditioning running can produce a monthly bill several times what an owner expects. It rises with occupancy, which means it rises when the villa earns, but it does not fall to nothing when empty.

## Reducing it

- Inverter air conditioning units rather than fixed speed
- Timers on the pool pump rather than continuous running
- Insulation and shading, which is a design decision made once
- Solar, which is genuinely viable here given the irradiance
- Guest guidance about doors and windows, which works better than it sounds

## Outages

Short outages are routine rather than exceptional. A villa operating commercially should have at least surge protection, and larger properties frequently run a generator or battery backup.

Guests forgive a ten-minute outage. They do not forgive a night without air conditioning in February.

## Common questions

### How much is electricity in Bali per month?
It depends entirely on air conditioning use and pool plant. A three-bedroom villa in high occupancy runs a bill many times that of a simple local house, and it scales with guest nights.

### What is PLN?
Perusahaan Listrik Negara, Indonesia's state electricity utility and the supplier for essentially all connections.

### What does VA mean on an Indonesian electricity connection?
It is the contracted capacity of the connection. Too little capacity means the breaker trips when several appliances run at once, and upgrading takes an application and a fee.

### Is solar worth it in Bali?
The irradiance is excellent and the economics work reasonably well for a property with a high, steady daytime load such as a pool pump and air conditioning. Confirm current metering and export rules before sizing a system.

### Is the power reliable in Bali?
Mostly, with short outages that are routine. A commercially operating villa should carry surge protection and consider backup.
