Published rates are peak-season asking rates. The number that matters is what a property achieves across twelve months.

## What drives rate, in order

**Outlook.** An unrepeatable view, cliff or ocean, is the single largest premium available. Uluwatu and the Bukit cliffs sit at the top of the island's range for this reason alone.

**Walkability.** Batu Bolong and Seminyak command premiums because guests can walk to dinner. In an island where two kilometres takes twenty-five minutes at six in the evening, that is a real product.

**Differentiation.** In corridors with hundreds of near-identical three-bedroom pool villas, the generic ones compete on price and the distinctive ones do not.

**Bedroom count and layout.** Groups pay more per head for larger villas, and the four-bedroom market is less crowded than the three.

## The month that decides the year

July and August fill themselves. December fills. The annual figure is decided by February, March and November, and those are the months sales material does not discuss.

Model month by month rather than applying an annual average, and reduce year one substantially because a new listing has no reviews and no ranking.

## Long-term rates

Annual leasing is a different market, frequently paid a year in advance. Canggu long-term rentals start from around IDR 17.5M a month and rise sharply for anything with a private pool near the beach.

For generic villas in oversupplied corridors, annual leasing often nets similarly to nightly with a fraction of the operating load.

## Common questions

### How much does a villa rent for in Bali?
It varies enormously by outlook, walkability and differentiation. Peak-season asking rates are not what a property achieves across a year.

### Which area has the highest villa rates?
Cliff and ocean-view properties on the Bukit, followed by walkable Seminyak and Batu Bolong.

### What occupancy should I assume?
Model month by month, reduce year one substantially for a new listing, then calculate break-even occupancy from your fixed costs.

### Is long-term rental better than nightly?
For generic villas in competitive corridors, frequently yes after costs, and with far less operational work.

### How much is a long-term villa rental in Canggu?
From around IDR 17.5M a month, rising sharply closer to the beach and with a private pool.
