KITAS, Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas, is the limited stay permit. People talk about it as though it is one document. It is a category.

## The types that matter

| Permit | For | Work allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E33G | Remote work for a foreign employer | Foreign income only |
| E28A investor | Shareholders in an Indonesian company | Depends on role and authorisation |
| Work KITAS | Employment by an Indonesian company | Yes, with the sponsoring employer |
| Family KITAS | Spouse and children of a sponsor | **No** |
| Retirement | Qualifying retirees | **No** |
| Second Home | Self-supporting long stay | **No** |

## The pattern people miss

**Most of these do not permit work.** Family KITAS, retirement and Second Home grant presence, not the right to earn.

Since the Dharma Dewata task force became operational on 15 April 2026, that distinction has been enforced. Roughly 100 officers across Canggu, Ubud, Seminyak, Kerobokan and Uluwatu detained 62 foreign nationals in the first three weeks, and the test applied is activity rather than payment.

Unpaid volunteering, yoga instruction, wellness workshops and sponsored social posts have all been treated as work.

## Why it matters for property

**Hak Pakai requires valid residency.** It is the only route that registers a land right in a foreigner's own name, and a KITAS is what unlocks it.

Leasehold and PT PMA routes do not require personal residency, which is why buyers without a permit use them.

## Choosing correctly

Start from what you will actually do here, not from how long each permit lasts. Then hold the evidence supporting it: contracts, company documents, marriage certificate legalised, whatever the basis is.

## KITAP

After qualifying consecutive years on an appropriate KITAS, or through marriage to an Indonesian citizen, **KITAP** grants permanent stay. Longer validity, fewer renewals, and a more stable footing for Hak Pakai.

The qualifying years generally need to be continuous, so switching categories can reset the count.

## Common questions

### What is a KITAS?
The Indonesian limited stay permit. It comes in several types covering remote work, investment, employment, family, retirement and long stay.

### Which KITAS allows you to work in Bali?
A work KITAS with a sponsoring employer, or an investor KITAS with the appropriate role and authorisation. E33G covers remote work for a foreign employer only.

### Can I work on a family KITAS?
No. It grants residence, not the right to earn, and that is being actively enforced.

### Do I need a KITAS to buy property in Bali?
For Hak Pakai in your own name, yes. Leasehold and PT PMA routes do not require personal residency.

### How do I get KITAP?
Through qualifying consecutive years on an appropriate KITAS, or through marriage to an Indonesian citizen. Continuity matters, so gaps can reset the clock.
