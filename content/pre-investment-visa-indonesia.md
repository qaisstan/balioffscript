This category exists for a real and common situation: you are considering putting money into Indonesia and need to be here properly while you decide.

## What it is for

Assessing investment opportunities. Meeting potential partners, developers, notaries and advisers. Inspecting sites and properties. Conducting the research that precedes forming a PT PMA or committing capital.

It comes in single entry and multiple entry forms. The multiple entry version suits anyone making several trips across a year, which is what a serious investment assessment usually takes.

## Why it fits property buyers

Most foreign buyers here spend months looking before committing. That involves repeat trips, notary meetings, site visits and negotiation, none of which is tourism and none of which is yet operating a business.

A pre-investment visa describes that position accurately, which matters if anyone asks why you are here.

## What it still does not permit

Working. Operating a business. Earning Indonesian-source income. It covers **assessing** an investment, not running one.

Once you form the company and take a role in it, you need an investor or work KITAS instead.

## Multiple entry mechanics

A multiple entry visa has a validity period and a **maximum stay per visit**. You can enter repeatedly across the validity window, but each individual stay is capped.

People conflate the two and overstay. Track the per-visit limit, not just the expiry date.

## The sensible sequence

1. Pre-investment visa while you research and negotiate
2. Form the PT PMA once you commit
3. Investor KITAS once the company exists and you hold the shareholding

Doing this in order avoids the common mistake of operating on a visitor visa because the company is "nearly ready".

## Common questions

### What is a pre-investment visa in Indonesia?
A visit visa for people assessing investment opportunities, covering meetings, site inspections and research before committing capital.

### Can I look at property on a pre-investment visa?
Yes. Assessing an investment, including property, is what the category is for.

### Can I work on a pre-investment visa?
No. It covers assessing an investment, not operating a business or earning income here.

### Single or multiple entry?
Multiple entry suits anyone making several trips across a year, which most serious investment assessments require.

### What do I move to after investing?
An investor KITAS once the PT PMA exists and you hold the qualifying shareholding.
