Foreign sellers look for a capital gains regime and do not find one, because Indonesia approaches disposals differently.

## Not a gain tax, a transaction tax

Most Western systems tax the **profit**: sale price minus purchase price minus costs.

Indonesia applies a **final tax on the transfer value** of the property. It is calculated on what the property sold for, not on what you made.

The practical consequences are significant.

**If you made a large gain**, this is usually favourable. The tax does not scale with your profit.

**If you made a loss**, you still pay. The tax applies to the transaction regardless of whether you did well.

**It is final.** Being a final tax, it generally settles the liability on that transaction rather than folding into a wider annual computation.

## Who bears it

The **seller** normally bears the tax on disposal, while the buyer bears BPHTB on acquisition.

In practice who pays what is negotiable and frequently negotiated. Whatever you agree, put it expressly in the deed. A silent deed creates an argument at the worst moment.

## Where a company holds the property

The treatment differs. A PT PMA selling an asset, or shareholders selling the company's shares, are different transactions with different tax positions.

This is one of the genuine planning considerations when choosing the structure at purchase, and it is worth taking advice on before you buy rather than when you sell.

## Your home country still matters

A final tax in Indonesia does not automatically settle your position at home. Most countries tax residents on worldwide gains, and a treaty then relieves double taxation, usually by credit.

Relief is not automatic and needs documentation. Take advice in both countries, because one adviser rarely covers both properly.

## What to do before selling

- Establish the tax position for your specific structure
- Agree in writing who bears what, and put it in the deed
- Keep the purchase documentation, because your cost base matters at home even if it does not here
- If a company holds it, bring the filings current, because a share sale invites scrutiny

## Common questions

### Does Indonesia have capital gains tax on property?
Not in the usual sense. It applies a final tax on the transfer value of the property rather than on the profit made.

### Who pays the tax when selling property in Bali?
The seller normally bears the disposal tax and the buyer bears BPHTB. Who actually pays is negotiable and should be explicit in the deed.

### Do I pay tax at home as well?
Possibly. Most countries tax residents on worldwide gains, with treaty relief usually given as a credit, claimed with documentation.

### Is it different if a company owns the property?
Yes. Selling an asset and selling company shares are different transactions with different treatment.

### Can I offset a loss?
A tax on transaction value does not scale with profit, so a loss does not remove the liability.
