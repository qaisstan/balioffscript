Between doing nothing and forming a PT PMA sits a structure most people have never heard of.

## What it is

A representative office lets a **foreign company** establish a legal presence in Indonesia for defined non-commercial purposes: market research, liaison, promotion, supervising the parent's interests.

It is an extension of the foreign parent, not a separate Indonesian company.

## The hard limit

**It cannot generate revenue.** No sales, no contracts producing income, no invoicing Indonesian customers.

The moment you want to trade, you need a PT PMA. That is not a grey area and it is the reason the structure is narrower than it first appears.

## Against a PT PMA

| | Representative office | PT PMA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can earn revenue | **No** | Yes |
| Capital threshold | None of the PMA kind | Substantial, per classification and location |
| Setup burden | Lighter | Heavier |
| Ongoing compliance | Lighter | LKPM, monthly and annual filings |
| Can hold land | No | Yes, HGB |
| Supports investor KITAS | No | Yes |
| Can employ | Limited | Yes |

## Where it genuinely fits

A foreign company that wants boots on the ground before committing. Market research, building relationships, supervising a supply chain, or coordinating with Indonesian partners while the commercial decision is still open.

For a property buyer, it is usually **not** the right structure, because holding land needs HGB and that needs a PT PMA.

## Where people get it wrong

Using it to avoid PT PMA capital requirements while actually trading. That is operating without the right licence, and the classification closures of July 2026 have not made the authorities more relaxed about structure.

If revenue is the plan, the representative office is a staging post, not a destination.

## Common questions

### What is a representative office in Indonesia?
A legal presence for a foreign company covering research, liaison and promotion, without the ability to earn revenue.

### Can a representative office make money in Indonesia?
No. It cannot sell, contract for income or invoice Indonesian customers. Trading requires a PT PMA.

### Is it cheaper than a PT PMA?
Lighter on capital and compliance, but it cannot trade, so the comparison only matters if you genuinely do not need revenue yet.

### Can a representative office buy property?
No. Holding land through a company requires HGB, which needs a PT PMA.

### When should I upgrade to a PT PMA?
When you intend to earn revenue, employ properly, hold land, or support an investor KITAS.
