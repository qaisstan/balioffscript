North Bali runs along the Buleleng coast: Lovina, Singaraja, Pemuteran and the stretch toward the west. It is a different island from the south in almost every respect.

## What is actually there

Black sand beaches, dolphins offshore at Lovina, world-class diving at Menjangan and Pemuteran, waterfalls inland, and dramatically fewer tourists than the south.

Singaraja is the old colonial capital and the second city of Bali, with a university and real local economy rather than a tourism economy.

## The price

Coastal land here is a fraction of Canggu, and often a fraction of Tabanan. It is the cheapest sea-view land you can buy on Bali.

That discount is the whole thesis, and it exists because demand has not followed.

## The honest risk

Guests are few, seasons are short, and the drive from the airport is two and a half to three hours over the mountains. That drive is the single biggest constraint on North Bali's development, and it has been for decades.

A proposed North Bali airport has been discussed for many years. Treat it as a possibility rather than a plan, and do not price land on it. Buying land against an infrastructure project that may not happen is speculation on a government decision.

## Who it works for

People buying a home rather than a business. Retirees who want space, quiet and low cost and do not need the south. Divers. Anyone with a genuinely long horizon and capital they will not need back.

## Who it does not

Anyone underwriting rental yield. Anyone who may need to sell within five years, because the buyer pool is thin and slow. Anyone with children needing international schools or a family needing quick hospital access.

## What to check

- Zoning, since much of the coast is agricultural
- Access rights and road condition, which vary enormously
- Water supply, which is better than the Bukit but still parcel-specific
- Realistic drive time to the airport at the hour you would actually travel
- Who the buyer is when you want to sell

## Common questions

### Is North Bali a good investment?
It is a long-horizon bet on infrastructure and demand that have not arrived. The land is genuinely cheap. The rental market is genuinely thin.

### How far is Lovina from the airport?
Roughly two and a half to three hours over the mountains, and longer in bad weather. This is the central constraint on the area.

### Is the North Bali airport being built?
It has been proposed and discussed for many years without being delivered. Do not price land on the assumption it happens.

### Can foreigners buy property in North Bali?
The same national rules apply as everywhere in Indonesia: leasehold, Hak Pakai with residency, or HGB through a PT PMA. No freehold in a foreign name.

### Is there rental demand in Lovina?
Limited and seasonal, oriented to diving and to travellers doing a circuit of the island rather than staying put. Underwrite it far more conservatively than the south.
