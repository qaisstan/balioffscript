People sell off-plan contracts for good reasons: plans change, money is needed elsewhere, or the project is slow and they want out. In Bali this is possible, but it works differently from selling a finished villa.

## What you are actually selling

Before handover you do not hold a finished villa. You hold a **contract with the developer**: the right to receive the villa and the lease, once built. Selling means **transferring that contract** to a new buyer, usually called an assignment.

## The developer's consent

Most Bali off-plan contracts say you **cannot transfer before completion without the developer's written approval**. Some add a transfer fee. Read that clause first, then ask the developer in writing. Do not list the villa until you have a clear answer.

## What decides the price

1. **Progress on site.** A villa with roof, windows and pool sells far better than a slab.
2. **Trust in the developer.** If the project is known to be late, buyers want a discount for the risk.
3. **Lease years left.** The lease clock may already be running, even before handover. Fewer years means a lower price.
4. **Today's prices nearby.** If similar new villas now sell for more than you paid, you may sell at a profit, even with a delay.

## Three ways to sell

1. **Back to the developer**, or swapped into another unit of theirs.
2. **Through agents**, as a resale of the contract, with full disclosure of the build status.
3. **To another buyer in the project**, who knows the developer already.

## Protect yourself in the sale

- Use a **notary** for the transfer, with the developer as a party.
- Make sure the new buyer takes over **all remaining payments**, and the developer confirms you are released.
- Keep copies of every payment you made, and of the developer's written consent.

## Common questions

### Do I need the developer's permission to sell?
In most contracts, yes, until the villa is finished and handed over. After handover, the lease terms decide.

### Will I lose money selling off-plan?
Not always. It depends on what you paid, how far the build is, and today's prices. A delayed project usually sells at a discount.

### Is selling off-plan legal in Indonesia?
Transferring your contract rights is normal when the contract allows it and the paperwork goes through a notary.

### How long does it take to sell an off-plan villa?
It varies a lot. A finished, well-located villa from a trusted developer sells fastest; a delayed project can take much longer.
