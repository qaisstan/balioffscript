People form a PT PMA for a project that then stalls, and simply stop paying attention. That is the expensive option.

## What happens if you do nothing

The company continues to exist. Reporting obligations continue accruing: quarterly investment activity reports, monthly tax filings including nil returns, annual corporate tax returns and financial statements.

Penalties compound. The NIB can be suspended. Directors and shareholders stay associated with a non-compliant entity, which surfaces later when they try to form another company or apply for a permit.

## Why it matters personally

If your KITAS was sponsored by that company, your residence status is tied to an entity that is falling out of compliance. That is not a theoretical link.

Anyone planning to do business in Indonesia again should care about their record.

## The proper route

Closing a company in Indonesia is a formal process, not a decision to stop filing. In broad terms it involves:

- A shareholders' resolution to dissolve
- Appointing a liquidator
- Settling liabilities, including tax
- Tax deregistration and clearance
- Announcing the liquidation as required
- Deregistering the NIB and licences
- Final approval and removal from the register

It takes months rather than weeks, and tax clearance is usually the longest part.

## Start before you leave

If you are leaving Indonesia, begin this before you go. Running it remotely, without a bank account, without a local address and without someone to sign things, is considerably harder.

The same applies to closing corporate bank accounts and terminating staff lawfully, which has its own severance obligations.

## The cheaper alternatives

**If the project is paused rather than dead**, keeping the company minimally compliant is usually cheaper than closing and reforming. Filings are a cost; penalties and a fresh incorporation are a bigger one.

**If someone will buy it**, a clean company with a good compliance record and valid classifications has real value, particularly given that several classifications closed to new foreign registration in July 2026. That is worth more than the shell it appears to be.

## Common questions

### Can I just abandon my PT PMA?
No. It keeps existing and accruing reporting obligations and penalties, and it follows you into future applications.

### How long does it take to close a PT PMA?
Months rather than weeks, with tax clearance typically the longest stage.

### What happens to my KITAS if the company closes?
An investor or work KITAS sponsored by that company is tied to it. Plan your own status before dissolving the entity.

### Is it cheaper to keep a dormant company or close it?
If the project may resume, minimal compliance is usually cheaper. If it is finished, close it properly before penalties compound.

### Can I sell my PT PMA instead?
Sometimes, and it can be worth more than you expect. A compliant company holding classifications now closed to new foreign registration has genuine value.
