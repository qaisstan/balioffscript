One hundred and thirty thousand US dollars is a useful benchmark because it sits near several thresholds at once.

## What it actually buys here

| Option | Realistic at this price |
| --- | --- |
| Leasehold land, inland or west coast | A decent plot with years on the clock |
| Leasehold villa, outer corridors | A small one or two bedroom, older stock |
| Apartment, strata title | A unit, with Hak Pakai possible if you qualify |
| Land plus build, Tabanan or inland | Plot plus a modest villa, if you supervise |
| Canggu or Seminyak villa | Not a chance |

The honest version: at this level you are buying either land somewhere early, or a small building somewhere established. Not both.

## Against the alternatives

Spain's golden visa wanted 500,000 euros before it closed. Portugal's property route is gone. Greece has raised thresholds sharply in prime areas. Dubai ties residency to a property value well above this.

So as a residency-adjacent number, USD 130,000 in Indonesia is genuinely cheap. As a property number, it is entry level in a market where entry level means compromise.

## The trap at this price point

The temptation is a small villa in a developed corridor, marketed on yield. That is exactly the segment with the most competing supply and the softest rates.

The better use of this money is usually land in an area you have verified, held while the corridor develops, or a plot plus a build you actually supervise.

Both require patience. Neither produces income in year one.

## What to add to the number

Transaction costs of roughly 7 to 8 percent. Furnishing at USD 25,000 to 45,000 if it is a villa for rental. A cash buffer for a slow first year. If you are building, contingency of at least 15 percent.

A buyer who spends the entire 130,000 on the asset has under-budgeted by a third.

## Common questions

### Can you buy a villa in Bali for $130,000?
Yes, in outer corridors or as older stock, typically small and leasehold. Not in Canggu or Seminyak.

### Does $130,000 get you a visa in Indonesia?
Property value features in the Second Home route. Thresholds have been revised, so verify the current figure before planning around it.

### Is $130,000 enough to start investing in Bali?
It is enough to buy something. Whether it is enough to buy something worth owning depends entirely on whether you verify the land and resist the yield pitch.

### What is better at this price, land or a villa?
Usually land in a verified location, or land plus a supervised build. Small villas at this price sit in the most competitive rental segment.

### What costs come on top?
Around 7 to 8 percent transaction costs, furnishing if you rent it, and a buffer for a slow first year. Budget roughly a third above the purchase price.
