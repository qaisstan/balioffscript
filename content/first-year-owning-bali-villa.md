Almost every disappointing Bali investment looks fine on paper and goes wrong in the same order.

## Months 1 to 3: setup costs you did not model

Furnishing to rental standard runs USD 25,000 to 45,000 for a three-bedroom villa. Photography, listing setup, staff hiring, utility connections and electrical capacity upgrades all land here.

If the property needed repairs a survey would have found, they land here too.

## Months 3 to 6: the listing does not perform

A new listing has no reviews and no ranking. Platforms surface established properties first.

Expect occupancy materially below the area average while the first ten reviews accumulate. Owners who modelled year one at stabilised occupancy run short of working capital around month eight.

This is normal and it is the most commonly omitted assumption in every projection you will be shown.

## Months 6 to 9: the cost base becomes real

Staff with BPJS at roughly 14 percent and the THR month. Platform commission. Management fees plus everything billed separately. Utilities that rise with occupancy. Laundry and consumables scaling with turnover. Pool chemicals, garden, repairs.

Gross revenue looks fine. What arrives is a different number.

## Months 9 to 12: the first real maintenance

Humidity, salt and UV work fast. Soft goods degrade in one to three years. Something in the pool plant or the air conditioning fails.

If you did not provision a sinking fund, this comes out of the year's profit.

## What separates owners who do well

- They budgeted 30 to 40 percent above the purchase price for setup and buffer
- They modelled year one well below stabilised occupancy
- They own their own listing account, not the manager's
- They have someone genuinely present and responsive
- They provisioned a sinking fund from month one

## The single most useful thing to do before buying

Get twelve consecutive months of actual operating statements from a genuinely comparable property. Not a projection, not an average, statements.

If nobody will show you one, that is the information.

## Common questions

### How long before a Bali villa makes money?
Expect a materially weaker first year while reviews and ranking build. Model year one well below the area average.

### What costs come after buying a Bali villa?
Furnishing at USD 25,000 to 45,000, photography and listing setup, staff hiring, utility upgrades and immediate repairs.

### Why is my Bali villa underperforming the projection?
Usually a new listing with no ranking, plus a cost base that included staff obligations, commissions and separately billed items the projection omitted.

### What is a sinking fund and how much?
An annual provision for the refurbishment cycle that runs roughly every five years in this climate. Without it, the refurbishment reads as a loss year.

### How much cash buffer do I need?
Enough to cover a slow first year of fixed costs. Owners who budget only the purchase price are short by roughly a third.
