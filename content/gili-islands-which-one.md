The Gilis are three small coral islands off the northwest coast of Lombok. No cars, no motorbikes, no dogs. You walk, cycle or take a horse cart, and you can circle any of them on foot.

## Gili Trawangan

The largest and busiest. A strip of bars, restaurants, dive shops and hotels along the east coast, a night market, and the only real nightlife in the group.

**Suits:** younger travellers, divers, anyone who wants a social island with things happening.

**Size:** about 3 by 2 kilometres; an hour and a half to walk round.

**The honest version:** the east strip is developed and busy, the west and north are quiet and beautiful. The famous party reputation is real but concentrated, and much of the island is calm.

## Gili Air

The middle one in every sense: some bars and restaurants, a good spread of accommodation, a local village, and quiet beaches on the north and west.

**Suits:** most people. Couples, families, divers, and anyone who wants somewhere to eat dinner without a scene.

**Size:** about 1.5 kilometres across; an hour to walk round.

**The honest version:** the best balance of the three, and the one I would send most people to.

## Gili Meno

The smallest and quietest, with a salt lake in the middle, a turtle hatchery, an underwater sculpture installation offshore, and very little else.

**Suits:** honeymooners, people who want to read a book for four days, snorkellers.

**Size:** about 2 by 1 kilometres; 45 minutes to walk round.

**The honest version:** genuinely quiet, with limited food options and mosquitoes near the salt lake. Bring a book and repellent.

## What they all have

**Turquoise water and white sand**, which is the reason people come and which Bali's main beaches do not have.

**Snorkelling with green turtles**, which are common and easily seen from the shore, particularly off Gili Air and Meno.

**Diving.** A cluster of sites around the three islands including Shark Point, Halik and Meno Wall, with turtles, reef sharks and good coral. The islands are a popular and cheap place to certify.

**No motor vehicles.** Bicycles and cidomo horse carts. The horse welfare question is a real one and has improved in recent years; walking or cycling is the choice if it bothers you.

**Sunsets over Bali's Agung** from the west coasts, and sunrise over Rinjani from the east.

## Getting there

Fast boats from **Padang Bai** on Bali's east coast, roughly 1.5 to 2.5 hours, and from **Sanur** and **Serangan**, longer. Also from **Bangsal** on Lombok, a 15 to 30-minute local boat, which is what you use if you are coming from Lombok or flying into Lombok airport.

Crossings are weather-dependent, and the open-sea stretch between Bali and the Gilis is exposed. Wet season crossings are rougher and cancellations happen.

## Practicalities

**Cash.** ATMs exist on Trawangan and Air and are unreliable. Bring rupiah.

**Water.** Fresh water is scarce and largely shipped or desalinated. Showers can be brackish.

**Medical care is minimal**, with clinics but no hospital. Serious cases go to Lombok or Bali, and a night evacuation is slow and expensive. This matters for divers.

**Malaria and dengue.** Mosquito precautions are worth taking, particularly on Meno.

**Alcohol.** Stick to beer and sealed bottles at established venues. Counterfeit spirits have caused deaths in Indonesia and the Gilis have had incidents.

## How long

Three nights on one island, or four to five if you want to island-hop, which is easy on the daily shuttle boats.

## Common questions

### Which Gili island is the best?
Gili Air for balance, Trawangan for nightlife and diving infrastructure, Meno for quiet.

### Are there cars on the Gili Islands?
No. Transport is by bicycle, on foot or by horse cart.

### How do you get from Bali to the Gilis?
Fast boat from Padang Bai, roughly 1.5 to 2.5 hours, or from Sanur. Also short local boats from Bangsal on Lombok.

### Can you see turtles in the Gilis?
Yes, green turtles are common and can be seen while snorkelling from shore, especially off Gili Air and Meno.

### Are the Gili Islands expensive?
Accommodation and food cost a little more than mainland Bali because everything is shipped in. Diving is cheap.
