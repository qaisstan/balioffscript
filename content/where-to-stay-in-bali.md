Bali is not a destination, it is nine of them, and they are separated by roads that move at 25 kilometres an hour. Choosing the wrong base is how people end up spending a holiday in a car, and it is entirely avoidable.

## The short answer

| You want | Stay in |
|---|---|
| Surf, cafes, a scene, people your own age | Canggu |
| Restaurants, shopping, beach clubs, walkable | Seminyak |
| Jungle, yoga, culture, cool nights | Ubud |
| Cliffs, world-class surf, dramatic views | Uluwatu and the Bukit |
| Calm shallow sea, families, quiet | Sanur |
| A resort that does the thinking for you | Nusa Dua |
| Seafood, sunsets, near the airport | Jimbaran |
| Cheap, busy, nightlife, first-time backpacking | Kuta and Legian |
| Rice fields, almost no tourism, real Bali | Sidemen, Munduk, Amed |

## Canggu

The centre of gravity for anyone under 40. Surf at Batu Bolong and Echo Beach, cafes built for laptops, gyms, night markets, and a beach club every few hundred metres. Also: heavy traffic on lanes built for scooters, constant construction, and a beach with strong currents and dark sand.

**Stay here if** you surf, work remotely, or want the social version of Bali. **Avoid if** you want quiet, or you are travelling with small children.

## Seminyak and Petitenget

The polished end. The best restaurant scene on the island, boutique shopping, beach clubs on a long sunset-facing beach, and hotels at every level. Walkable by Bali standards, which is rare and valuable.

**Stay here if** you want to eat well and walk to dinner. **Avoid if** you want anything to feel undiscovered.

## Ubud

Inland, green, cooler, and culturally the heart of the island. Rice terraces, river gorges, temples, yoga, spas, the monkey forest, and a restaurant scene that rivals the south. It rains more, and it is 90 minutes from a beach.

**Stay here if** you want culture, nature and wellness. **Avoid if** you came for the sea.

## Uluwatu and the Bukit

Limestone cliffs, turquoise water, the best waves in Indonesia, and the most dramatic hotels. Beaches are at the bottom of long staircases, everything is far from everything else, and water supply and traffic are both strained.

**Stay here if** you surf seriously or want the view. **Avoid if** you have mobility limits or small children.

## Sanur

The east coast, sunrise side. A protected reef lagoon with calm shallow water, a flat beachfront path for walking and cycling, older visitors, and fast access to the airport and to the Nusa Islands. Unfashionable, which keeps it pleasant.

**Stay here if** you have children, you want to swim rather than surf, or you want an easy base. **Avoid if** you want nightlife.

## Nusa Dua

A planned resort enclave with manicured grounds, big international hotels, a calm swimming beach and the toll road to the airport. Almost nothing outside the resorts.

**Stay here if** you want a resort holiday with no logistics. **Avoid if** you want to see Bali.

## Jimbaran

Between the airport and the Bukit. A long calm-water bay, seafood grills on the sand at sunset, a mix of big resorts and small guesthouses, and 20 minutes from arrivals.

**Stay here if** you want calm water and a short transfer. **Avoid if** you want to walk to a scene.

## Kuta and Legian

Where Bali tourism started. Cheap accommodation, a long surf beach with lifeguards and gentle beginner waves, and loud nightlife. Busy, worn and much less fashionable than it was.

**Stay here if** you are on a budget, learning to surf, or in transit. **Avoid if** you are looking for the Bali in the photographs.

## The quiet east, north and centre

**Sidemen** for rice terraces under Mount Agung and almost no tourism. **Amed** for black sand, calm water and the best diving on the mainland. **Munduk and Bedugul** for cool highland forest, waterfalls and lakes. **Lovina** for the quiet north coast. **Pemuteran and Menjangan** for the best snorkelling in Bali.

**Stay here if** you have more than ten days or you have been before. **Avoid as a first base** if the trip is short.

## How to structure a trip

- **5 nights:** one base. Canggu, Seminyak or Ubud.
- **7 to 10 nights:** two bases. One inland or cliff, one beach.
- **14 nights:** three bases, with the third in the east or north, or an island.

Every move costs half a day. Two moves in a week is a trip about driving.

## Common questions

### What is the best area to stay in Bali for first-timers?
Seminyak or Canggu for the south, with three or four nights in Ubud. That covers beach, food and culture without long drives.

### Where should I stay in Bali for a quiet holiday?
Sanur, Amed, Sidemen or Munduk. Nusa Dua if you want quiet with resort facilities.

### Is Canggu or Seminyak better?
Canggu for surf, cafes and a younger scene. Seminyak for restaurants, shopping and walkability.

### How many areas should I stay in on a 10-day Bali trip?
Two, or at most three. Each change of base costs half a day in traffic.

### Where is the best beach area in Bali?
For swimming, Sanur, Jimbaran and Nusa Dua. For surf and scene, Canggu and Seminyak. For scenery, the Bukit cliffs.
