A spouse KITAS is the most stable residency route available to a foreigner in Indonesia, because it is not tied to a job. The extension still has to be done properly and on time.

## What the extension requires

Applied for **before expiry**, through your Indonesian spouse as sponsor.

Evidence that the basis still holds: the marriage subsisting, the sponsor's documents current, and the address record accurate.

The sponsor's **identity documents and family card** are part of the application, which means their paperwork being in order is part of your immigration position.

## Start early

A month to six weeks before expiry.

Spouse extensions depend on documents from third parties and on your sponsor's availability to sign and attend. A spouse travelling, working away, or simply busy in the week your permit expires is a problem you created by starting late.

Diarise the expiry at issue and diarise a start date a month before it.

## Where extensions fail

**Address record mismatches.** If you moved and did not update the **SKTT** and the permit record, the inconsistency surfaces here. Systems match strictly and reject rather than query.

**Sponsor document problems.** An expired ID, an out-of-date family card, or a marriage that was never properly reported and registered in Indonesia where it took place abroad. That last one catches couples who married overseas and assumed it was recognised automatically.

**Name inconsistencies** between the passport, the marriage certificate and earlier records.

**An agent who did not file.** Get the filing reference and receipt, not a reassurance.

## If the marriage ends

The permit is sponsored by the marriage. If the marriage ends by divorce or death, the basis for the permit ends with it.

There are rules about the period in which you must regularise your status or leave, and they are not generous. This is a genuinely difficult moment to be dealing with immigration administration, which is a reason to understand the position before it arises rather than during.

**Options may include** changing to a different permit type with a different basis — employment, investment, or a route based on children who are Indonesian citizens — and each takes time to arrange.

Take advice early rather than waiting to see.

## Toward KITAP

Continuous holding of a spouse KITAS for a qualifying period is the most common route to **KITAP**, the permanent stay permit, with longer validity and simpler renewals.

**"Continuous" does real work.** A gap caused by a late extension can reset the clock, which is a further reason to treat renewal dates seriously if permanent status is the objective.

Verify the current qualifying period, as it differs between categories and has changed.

## The thing that matters more than the permit

If you are married to an Indonesian citizen, the **prenuptial or postnuptial agreement** is more consequential than the visa.

Indonesian marital property rules treat assets acquired during a marriage as jointly held. Because a foreigner cannot hold Hak Milik, that joint interest compromises your Indonesian spouse's ability to hold freehold in their own country.

A registered agreement separating the estates fixes it, and since the Constitutional Court decision in 2015 it can be made after the wedding as well as before.

Couples routinely sort the visa and leave this. It is the wrong order — the visa affects where you can live, the agreement affects whether your spouse can own land.

## Work

A spouse KITAS is a **residence permit, not work authorisation**. Employment with an Indonesian company still requires the employer to obtain RPTKA approval and the corresponding permit.

There is a widely repeated belief that a spouse permit allows free working. Verify it against the current rules rather than acting on it, because the consequences fall on you and on your spouse as sponsor.

## Common questions

### How do you extend a spouse KITAS in Indonesia?
Before expiry, through your Indonesian spouse as sponsor, with evidence the marriage subsists, the sponsor's documents current and the address record accurate.

### When should I apply to extend a spouse KITAS?
A month to six weeks before expiry, because the application depends on your sponsor's documents and availability rather than only on your own.

### What happens to my KITAS if my marriage ends?
The basis for the permit ends with the marriage, and there are rules about the period in which you must regularise your status or leave. Take advice early.

### Why do spouse KITAS extensions get refused?
Address record mismatches, sponsor document problems, a marriage contracted abroad that was never reported and registered in Indonesia, and name inconsistencies across documents.

### Does a spouse KITAS let me work in Indonesia?
It is a residence permit. Employment with an Indonesian company still requires RPTKA approval and the corresponding permit, so verify the position before starting work.
