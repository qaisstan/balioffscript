There is more than one way to be paid by a property here, and the obvious one is the hardest.

## The four routes

**One: nightly rental.** Highest gross revenue, highest costs, licensed activity, and the most competitive segment in the market. Staff, laundry, commission, management and a five-year refurbishment cycle all come out of it.

**Two: annual leasing.** Lower headline rate, effectively full occupancy while leased, frequently paid a year in advance, almost no operating load. On a generic villa in an oversupplied corridor this often nets similarly with a fraction of the work.

**Three: land appreciation.** Buy well-zoned land in a corridor before it develops and hold. No income, no operating costs, no licence required. This is where the largest gains here have actually been made, and it requires patience and capital you do not need.

**Four: build and sell.** Buy land, build, sell the completed asset. The margin is real and so is the execution risk: permits, supervision, cost overruns and a buyer pool at the end.

## Which one suits which buyer

| You are | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Present, hands-on, differentiated property | Nightly rental |
| Absentee, want predictability | Annual leasing |
| Long horizon, capital not needed | Land |
| Experienced, willing to supervise | Build and sell |

## The mistake

Underwriting route one while living somewhere else. Nightly rental is a business with staff, guests, reviews and a licence. Run remotely through a manager you did not properly vet, it produces the gap between the projection and the bank statement.

## The licensing dimension

Routes one and four intersect with licensing hardest. Since 22 July 2026 the villa and homestay classifications are closed to new foreign-owned company registration, which changes how a new project structures its operating side.

Routes two and three are considerably less exposed to that.

## Common questions

### How do foreigners make money from Bali property?
Nightly rental, annual leasing, land appreciation, or building and selling. Most people only consider the first.

### Is long-term rental better than Airbnb in Bali?
Often, after costs. Short-term produces higher gross and far higher operating expense, vacancy and wear.

### Can you make money from land in Bali without building?
Yes, and historically that is where the largest gains have been. It requires verified zoning, a long horizon and no need for income in the meantime.

### What is the most profitable Bali property strategy?
There is no single answer. The most profitable route is the one matching your presence, patience and tolerance for running a business.

### Do I need a licence to earn from Bali property?
For nightly accommodation, yes. Annual leasing sits under a different regime and is generally simpler.
