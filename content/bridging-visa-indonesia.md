Most overstays are not deliberate. They happen in the gap between one status ending and the next beginning.

## When the gap appears

Moving from a visit visa to a KITAS. Switching from work to investor. Changing sponsor. Converting to KITAP. Each involves an old status ending and a new one being issued, and the two do not always meet cleanly.

## Why it happens

The new application depends on documents that take time: a company's filings brought current, a marriage certificate legalised abroad, an RPTKA approval, a sworn translation.

Meanwhile the existing permit runs down on its own schedule, indifferent to how your paperwork is going.

## What to do about it

**Start the new application absurdly early.** Weeks, not days. The foreign parts of any document chain run on another country's timetable.

**Ask your agent or sponsor directly**: what is my status on the day the current permit expires, and what document evidences it? A vague answer is the warning.

**Do not leave and re-enter to solve it** without confirming that is the correct route. A new entry can cancel a pending application and put you further back.

**Keep evidence of the pending application.** If your status is covered while processing, you want the document proving it in your hand, not a verbal assurance.

## The cost of getting it wrong

Daily fines from the first day. Longer overstays escalate toward detention, deportation and a multi-year re-entry ban, which applies to Indonesia as a whole.

It also goes on your immigration record and affects later applications, including for residence permits.

## The continuity point

If you are working toward **KITAP**, a gap can reset your qualifying period. Years of consecutive residence can be undone by a fortnight of poor planning.

That is the expensive version of this mistake and people discover it years later at the application.

## Common questions

### What happens if my Indonesian visa expires while I am applying for a new one?
It depends on the route and whether your status is covered during processing. Confirm with your sponsor and get the evidence in writing.

### Is there a bridging visa in Indonesia?
Arrangements exist for gaps in some circumstances. Do not assume one applies to you; confirm before your current permit expires.

### Can I leave and re-enter to fix a gap?
Sometimes, but a new entry can cancel a pending application. Confirm the correct route before flying.

### How early should I apply to change category?
Weeks ahead. Documents needing foreign legalisation and translation run on someone else's timetable.

### Does a gap affect my path to permanent stay?
Yes. A lapse in permit history can reset the qualifying period for KITAP.
