People plan the arrival carefully and improvise the departure. The departure is the part that affects whether you can come back cleanly.

## What an EPO is

Exit Permit Only. Where you hold a KITAS and intend to leave permanently rather than return, the permit is formally cancelled and an exit permit issued, allowing you to depart with your status properly closed.

Historically this was a distinct document. The process and terminology have been simplified and changed over time, so confirm the current requirement rather than relying on how it worked for someone a few years ago.

## Why it matters

An unclosed permit is an open record. It can create difficulties when you next apply for an Indonesian visa, complicate the sponsor's position, and leave loose ends around tax registration and company roles.

If your KITAS was sponsored by a company or a spouse, leaving without closing it also leaves obligations sitting with that sponsor.

## What else to close

Leaving properly is more than immigration.

| Item | Action |
| --- | --- |
| KITAS or KITAP | Cancel and obtain the exit permit |
| Tax | Deregister or file appropriately for your NPWP |
| Company | Resign roles, or close the PT PMA properly if it is dormant |
| Staff | Terminate lawfully, with severance calculated correctly |
| Bank accounts | Close, or understand what happens when your permit lapses |
| Utilities and leases | Give notice and settle |
| Property | Decide whether to sell, lease out, or appoint a manager |

## The company you leave behind

A dormant PT PMA does not stop existing because you left. Reporting obligations continue accruing, penalties compound, and the eventual cost of regularising or closing it exceeds maintaining it would have.

If the venture is over, close the company properly. That takes time, so start before you leave rather than after.

## Property held while non-resident

Hak Pakai requires valid residency. If you hold it and your permit lapses, there are consequences to understand rather than discover.

Leasehold and property held through a company are less affected, which is one of the practical reasons buyers who may not stay long favour those structures.

## Common questions

### What is an EPO in Indonesia?
Exit Permit Only, the mechanism for formally cancelling a stay permit when leaving Indonesia permanently. Process and terminology have changed, so confirm the current requirement.

### What happens if I leave Indonesia without cancelling my KITAS?
The permit remains open on your record, which can complicate future applications and leaves obligations with your sponsor.

### Do I need to close my Indonesian bank account when I leave?
Accounts are tied to your permit status. Close them deliberately or understand what happens when the permit lapses, rather than leaving it unresolved.

### What happens to my PT PMA if I leave Indonesia?
It continues to exist and to accrue reporting obligations and penalties. If the venture is finished, close it properly, and start the process before you leave.

### Can I keep my Bali property if I leave Indonesia?
Leasehold and company-held property are generally unaffected. Hak Pakai depends on valid residency, so understand the position before your permit lapses.
