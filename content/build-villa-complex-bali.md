Five or ten villas around a shared entrance, a reception and a garden: on paper it is the most efficient way to build in Bali. In practice, a complex is a small business, and it needs to be set up like one.

## Step 1: the land and the zone

A complex rents to guests, so it is **tourism accommodation**. The plot must be in a zone that allows it. Check:

- the **zoning colour** and what it permits;
- the **building coverage and floor limits** (KDB/KLB) and the **15-metre height limit**;
- that it is not **agricultural land**, where building is banned across Bali;
- whether the regency is one of the six under the **2025 moratorium** on new accommodation permits (Tabanan, Jembrana, Buleleng, Bangli, Karangasem, Klungkung).

A plot that allows two villas but not a complex is the most expensive mistake in this list.

## Step 2: the structure

Renting rooms to guests is a business. As a foreigner you need a **PT PMA** with business codes that cover short-stay accommodation, or an Indonesian partner structure that is real, not a nominee. In 2026 Bali restricted new PT PMA registrations in several codes, so confirm what is open before you buy land.

The PT PMA can hold the land on **HGB** or lease it from the owner.

## Step 3: permits

- **PBG** (building permit) for the whole complex, before building.
- **SLF** (certificate of function) before guests arrive.
- The **tourism business licence** for the type of accommodation, plus tax registration for the local hotel and restaurant tax.

## Step 4: shared infrastructure

What makes a complex work, and what first-time builders under-budget: the internal road, parking, drainage, a septic or wastewater system sized for all units, water supply, power capacity from PLN, staff areas, storage and laundry.

## Step 5: rent or sell

- **Rent and keep:** one company, one manager, one brand. Higher income, more work.
- **Sell the units:** each buyer gets a lease or sub-lease. Then you need clear rules for the shared areas, a management agreement, and a service charge everyone pays. Without them, the complex falls apart after a few years.

## Common questions

### Can I build several villas on one plot in Bali?
Yes, if the zoning and building limits allow it and the plot is not agricultural land. Check before you buy.

### Do I need a company to run a villa complex?
To rent to guests legally, yes. As a foreigner that means a PT PMA with the right business codes.

### How long does it take to build a villa complex in Bali?
Permits plus construction usually take well over a year. Plan for weather, materials and permit delays.

### Can I sell the villas in my complex to foreigners?
As leases or sub-leases, through a notary, with a management agreement for the shared areas.
