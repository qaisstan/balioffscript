The assumption that platform income sitting in a foreign account is outside the Indonesian system is both common and wrong.

## Where the income arises

Income from a property in Indonesia is **Indonesian-source income**. It does not matter that the guest booked on a foreign platform, paid in another currency, or that the money landed in your account at home.

The villa is here. The income arose here.

## What you owe depends on structure

| How you hold it | Broad treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Non-resident individual | Withholding on gross Indonesian-source income |
| Resident individual | Progressive rates, NPWP required to file |
| PT PMA | Corporate tax on profit, with deductible operating costs |

The difference matters enormously for a villa, because operating costs consume a large share of revenue.

Where a flat rate applies to **gross** receipts, your staff, management fees, commission, utilities and maintenance do not reduce the tax at all. Where profit is taxed, they do.

That is one of the strongest legitimate arguments for running a genuine rental operation through a properly constituted company rather than personally.

## The licence question sits underneath all of it

Nightly accommodation is a licensed commercial activity. Bali closed the villa and homestay classifications to new foreign-owned company registration on 22 July 2026.

Paying tax on income from an unlicensed operation does not make the operation lawful. The two are separate obligations and you need both.

## Traceability

Payment flows are considerably more visible than they were. Assuming platform income is invisible is not a strategy, it is a deferred problem with penalties attached.

Budget tax as an operating cost from the first projection. A model showing net yield without it is showing you a number that does not exist.

## Practical steps

- Establish your structure before you start letting, not after
- Register for an NPWP if you hold property or a company here
- Keep contemporaneous records of income and every operating cost
- Reconcile platform statements to what actually arrived
- Take local advice on whether the gross or profit basis applies to you

## Common questions

### Do I pay tax on Airbnb income in Bali?
Yes. Income from an Indonesian property is Indonesian-source income regardless of where the guest paid or where the money landed.

### How much tax do you pay on rental income in Indonesia?
It depends on whether you are taxed as a non-resident, a resident, or through a company, and the three differ substantially.

### Can I deduct my villa costs against rental income?
Only where profit is taxed rather than gross receipts. This is a major reason operators use a properly constituted company.

### Does Airbnb report to Indonesian tax authorities?
Payment traceability has improved considerably. Assuming platform income is invisible is not a plan.

### Do I need a licence as well as paying tax?
Yes. They are separate obligations. Paying tax does not make an unlicensed operation lawful.
