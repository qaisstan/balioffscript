Investment and living are different questions. This one is only about where money works.

## The trade, area by area

| Area | Entry price | Rental demand | Resale depth | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canggu, Berawa | High | Deepest on the island | Deepest | Liquidity, proven demand |
| Seminyak | Highest | Strong, mature | Deep | Operating asset, walkability |
| Pererenan, Cemagi | High | Strong, heavy new supply | Good | Newer stock, corridor growth |
| Uluwatu, Bukit | High for views | Top nightly rates, seasonal | Moderate | Premium rates, view scarcity |
| Sanur | Moderate | Steady, long-stay | Moderate | Occupancy over rate |
| Ubud, Gianyar | Moderate | Less seasonal, lower ceiling | Moderate | Wellness and long stays |
| Tabanan, west | Lowest coastal | Thin | Thin | Land banking, patience |
| North and east | Lowest | Very thin | Very thin | Lifestyle, long horizon |

## The two strategies

**Income now.** Buy where guests already are, accept the entry price, and compete on differentiation rather than price. Canggu, Seminyak and the Bukit view properties.

**Upside later.** Buy verified land ahead of demand and hold. Tabanan and the west coast. No income, lower price, and your return depends on infrastructure arriving.

Trying to do both at once is how people end up with a generic villa in an oversupplied corridor: too expensive to be a land play, too undifferentiated to fill a calendar.

## What actually decides returns within an area

Not the area. Whether the specific property is one of two hundred near-identical three-bedroom pool villas, or has something unrepeatable: a view that cannot be built out, a plot size the market is not building, a walkable position.

Generic product competes on price wherever it is.

## The checks that change valuation

Zoning on the parcel, remaining term and extension clause, whether the operating licence exists and transfers, PBG and SLF, access road width, and water supply. Each one moves the number materially.

## Common questions

### Which area of Bali has the best rental yield?
Headline rates are highest in Uluwatu and Canggu. Annual yield after vacancy, management and maintenance is often closer between areas than marketing suggests.

### Where is the cheapest place to invest in Bali?
Tabanan and the west coast, then inland Gianyar. Both trade lower price for thinner demand and more zoning constraints.

### Is Canggu still worth investing in?
It has the deepest demand and the only reliable resale market, alongside the most competing supply. Differentiated property still works; generic does not.

### Should I buy land or a villa in Bali?
Land if you have a long horizon and no need for income. A villa if you want cash flow and will treat it as a business with a licence.

### What makes one Bali property outperform another in the same area?
Differentiation. An unrepeatable view, an unusual plot size, or a walkable location beats another identical pool villa regardless of the postcode.
