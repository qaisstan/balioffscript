Yes, legally, on a defined route. The problem is that most people work here on a tourist status, which is not a grey area.

## The right permit

**E33G** covers a foreign national who stays employed by, or contracted to, a company established outside Indonesia, and wants residence here without becoming locally employed.

The published criteria are specific:

- A genuine employment or consulting agreement with a **foreign** company
- Minimum annual income commonly cited at **USD 60,000**
- General living funds, commonly cited around USD 2,000
- **No income from Indonesian entities at all**

It runs one year and renews while the arrangement and the income continue.

## What it does not cover

It is not a general freelancer permit. If you cannot document both the foreign employment relationship and the income level, you likely do not qualify, whatever an agent tells you.

It also does not permit any Indonesian-sourced income. Take on a local client and you are outside the terms.

## The mistake almost everyone makes

Working on a visitor status. A visa on arrival gives 30 days plus one extension. It permits tourism and meetings. It does not permit work, and the fact that your employer is abroad does not change that.

This is enforced. It is not a technicality.

## The part nobody plans for

**Tax residence follows presence, not your visa.** Past 183 days in a year you are an Indonesian tax resident, even though every dollar you earn is foreign.

This is the single most misunderstood point among remote workers here, and it is worth a conversation with a tax adviser before you cross that line rather than after.

## If you are considering property

Buying does not give you the right to stay, and the E33G is not framed as a route to permanent residence. If long-term security is the goal, [look at the routes that do lead there](/visas/kitap-itap-indonesia/) before you commit capital.

## Common questions

### Is there a digital nomad visa for Bali?
Yes. The E33G permit covers people who remain employed by or contracted to a company established outside Indonesia and want to live here without working locally.

### What are the E33G requirements?
A genuine employment or consulting agreement with a foreign company, a minimum annual income commonly cited at USD 60,000, general living funds of around USD 2,000, and no income from Indonesian entities.

### How long is the E33G valid?
One year, renewable while the foreign employment relationship and the income threshold continue to be met.

### Can freelancers get the E33G?
Only if they can document both the foreign employment relationship and the published income level. An independent consultant with clear foreign contracts may qualify, but it should be checked against the current official criteria rather than assumed.

### Do digital nomads pay tax in Indonesia?
Tax residence follows physical presence. Beyond 183 days in a year you are an Indonesian tax resident, even though your income is foreign and paid abroad.
