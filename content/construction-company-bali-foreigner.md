Bali is full of building sites, and plenty of foreigners with building experience see a business in it. You can set one up, but construction is one of the more tightly licensed sectors in Indonesia.

## The company

A foreigner needs a **PT PMA** with construction business codes. On top of the usual PT PMA rules (**IDR 2.5 billion** paid up, an investment plan above **IDR 10 billion per code per location**), construction has its own layer:

- **SBU (construction business certificate).** Without it, a construction company cannot legally carry out projects or sign construction contracts.
- **Qualification level.** Foreign-owned construction companies are generally placed in the **large** qualification, which comes with requirements on experience, staff and equipment.
- **Certified staff.** You need Indonesian engineers and technicians with the right competence certificates.

Foreign ownership limits for construction have been moving with new regulations, so check the current cap before you set up.

## What you can and cannot do

A foreign-owned contractor in the large qualification is aimed at **bigger projects**. Small residential jobs are largely the space of local contractors. That is why many foreigners in Bali do not build a construction company at all. They:

- run a **design or project management company** and hire licensed local contractors;
- or partner with an established Indonesian contractor, on a proper contract.

## Working with people

You need a **work permit (KITAS)** to work in your own company, and the company must sponsor it. Your crew will be Indonesian, and labour rules (BPJS, THR, contracts) apply to everyone you employ.

## If you just want to build your own villas

You do not need a construction company. You need a good **contractor contract**, a **quantity surveyor**, staged payments tied to progress, and someone checking the site every week.

## Common questions

### Can a foreigner own a construction company in Indonesia?
Yes, as a PT PMA with an SBU, usually in the large qualification. Ownership limits for construction have changed recently; confirm the current rule.

### What is an SBU in Indonesia?
The construction business certificate. Without it, a company cannot legally carry out construction work or contracts.

### Can I build villas for clients in Bali as a foreigner?
Through a licensed company, yes. Many foreigners instead manage projects and use licensed local contractors.

### Do I need a work permit to run my construction company?
Yes. Working in Indonesia, including in your own company, requires a KITAS sponsored by the company.
