Bali real estate works differently from almost every market foreign buyers come from. This is the whole picture in one page.

## 1. What you can hold

No freehold. Three lawful routes: a **leasehold** contract, **Hak Pakai** as a registered right of use if you hold residency, or **HGB** held by your **PT PMA**. Anything else offered to you is a nominee arrangement, which is prohibited and gives you nothing enforceable.

## 2. What it costs to transact

| Item | Typical |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer transfer duty on a titled transfer | Around 5 percent |
| Notary and PPAT fees | 1 to 2.5 percent |
| Independent legal review | The cheapest line and the most skipped |
| **Total** | **Budget 7 to 8 percent** |

## 3. What it costs to build

USD 600 to 1,500 per square metre by specification. A pool is USD 15,000 to 50,000 separately. Furnishing a three-bedroom villa to rental standard is USD 25,000 to 45,000. Quotes routinely exclude pool, landscaping, furniture, air conditioning, fees and utility connections.

## 4. What it costs to run

Staff for a three-bedroom villa run IDR 84M to 100M a year once BPJS at roughly 14 percent and the mandatory THR month are counted. Then management, platform commission, utilities, repairs, insurance, tax, and a sinking fund for the refurbishment every five years.

## 5. What it earns

Gross yields are quoted high and net yields land far lower once vacancy, commission, management, staff, maintenance, tax and refurbishment are subtracted. Calculate **break-even occupancy**: annual fixed costs divided by what one booked night actually contributes.

## 6. What you must verify

- Certificate at the land office, through a notary **you** appointed
- Seller is the registered owner, spouse and heirs consenting
- Zoning on the specific parcel permits your intended use
- Boundaries walked against the certificate
- Access rights registered, not customary
- PBG and SLF describe the building as it stands
- Operating licence exists, covers this building, and transfers

## 7. The order that protects you

Structure first. Then what it can lawfully earn. Then verification. Then price. Areas and photographs last.

Buyers who reverse this pay a deposit on something beautiful and discover the zoning afterwards.

## Common questions

### Can foreigners buy real estate in Bali?
Not freehold. Through a leasehold, Hak Pakai with residency, or HGB held by a PT PMA.

### How much does property cost in Bali?
Enormously variable by area and title. Whatever the price, add roughly 7 to 8 percent in transaction costs plus fit-out and a buffer.

### Is Bali real estate a good investment?
Well-located, well-zoned land with a lawful structure and a real licence still works. Generic villas priced on optimistic projections increasingly do not.

### What is the first step to buying in Bali?
Decide the ownership structure, because it determines which properties are even available to you.

### Do I need a lawyer to buy in Bali?
You need an independent notary you appointed, and independent legal review. Not the seller's.
