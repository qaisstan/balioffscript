Indonesian property law for foreigners comes down to a small number of rules, applied consistently. Here they are.

## Rule 1: land is reserved for citizens

The Basic Agrarian Law reserves **Hak Milik**, freehold, to Indonesian citizens. This is long-standing and constitutional in character. It is not worked around.

## Rule 2: foreigners hold use rights, not ownership

Three lawful instruments. **Hak Sewa**, a lease contract. **Hak Pakai**, a registered right of use requiring residency and meeting minimum value conditions. **HGB**, a registered building right held by an Indonesian legal entity such as a PT PMA.

## Rule 3: nominee arrangements are prohibited

Holding land through an Indonesian nominee is unlawful. The supporting side agreements are unenforceable because their purpose is circumvention, and since **Perda Bali 4/2026** facilitating such an arrangement is itself an offence, which reaches the advisers who arrange them.

## Rule 4: rights are term-limited and expiry is real

Every route available to a foreigner runs out. Registered titles have extension and renewal paths with unforgiving deadlines. A lease has only what its contract gives it.

## Rule 5: holding, building and operating are three separate permissions

Holding land does not permit you to build. Building requires **PBG** before construction and **SLF** on completion, within the zoning and the KDB, KLB, height and setback limits for that parcel.

Operating short-term accommodation is a further, separately licensed activity. On 22 July 2026 Bali closed villa and homestay classifications to new foreign-owned company registration.

## Rule 6: marriage does not create an exception

Property acquired during a marriage is joint marital property by default. A foreigner married to an Indonesian citizen, without a prenuptial or postnuptial agreement separating assets, creates a genuine problem: the foreign spouse acquires an interest in a title they cannot lawfully hold.

## What follows from all six

Verification is the entire job. Certificate checked at the land office through a notary you appointed. Zoning on the specific parcel. Boundaries walked. Access rights registered. PBG and SLF present. Licence confirmed and transferable.

Every loss story in this market is one of these six rules being ignored, usually after a deposit was already paid.

## Common questions

### What is the law on foreigners buying property in Bali?
The Basic Agrarian Law reserves freehold to Indonesian citizens. Foreigners may hold a lease, Hak Pakai with residency, or HGB through an Indonesian company.

### Is it illegal for a foreigner to own land in Indonesia?
A foreigner cannot hold Hak Milik. Using a nominee to appear to do so is prohibited, and facilitating that is an offence in Bali under Perda Bali 4/2026.

### What is Perda Bali 4/2026?
A Bali regional regulation prohibiting nominee land arrangements and making facilitation an offence, with powers to warn, suspend operations and close premises.

### Do I need a permit to build in Bali?
Yes. PBG before construction and SLF on completion, within the zoning and coverage limits applying to the parcel.

### Can my Indonesian spouse hold land for me?
Not as a workaround. Without an asset separation agreement, marital property rules create a problem rather than solving one.
