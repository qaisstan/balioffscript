This is the category that catches the most well-intentioned people, because the assumption that unpaid means unregulated is wrong.

## The rule that surprises people

**Payment is not the test. Activity is.**

Since the Dharma Dewata task force became operational in April 2026, unpaid volunteering, yoga instruction, wellness workshops and unpaid teaching have all been treated as work. Roughly 100 officers across the main expat areas detained 62 foreign nationals in the first three weeks.

You can be deported for an activity you were not paid a rupiah for.

## The proper route

Indonesia has a **volunteer visit visa** category. It requires a sponsoring organisation, usually the foundation, NGO or institution you will be working with.

That sponsor requirement is the point. It ties the activity to a recognised organisation that takes responsibility for it, which is what makes the volunteering lawful rather than informal.

## What counts as volunteering

Teaching at a school or community project. Working with an NGO or foundation. Environmental or conservation work. Construction or renovation on a charitable project. Animal welfare work.

If an organisation is directing your time and you are producing something for them, that is the category, regardless of money.

## The yoga and wellness problem

Teaching a class, running a workshop or holding a retreat is work. So is doing it for free, for exposure, or in exchange for accommodation.

Accommodation in exchange for teaching is **compensation**. So is a free villa stay in exchange for promotion. The absence of cash does not place an activity outside the definition.

## What to do

Ask the organisation which visa they sponsor volunteers on. A legitimate foundation will know and will have done it before.

If they tell you a tourist visa is fine, that is their risk assessment, not yours, and you are the one who gets deported and banned.

## Common questions

### Do I need a visa to volunteer in Indonesia?
Yes. There is a volunteer visit visa category requiring a sponsoring organisation. Volunteering on a tourist visa is a violation.

### Is unpaid work still illegal in Bali?
Yes. The enforcement test is the activity, not whether you were paid.

### Can I teach yoga in Bali for free?
No. Teaching is work whether or not you are paid, and it has been specifically targeted in enforcement.

### What if accommodation is provided instead of pay?
That is compensation. It does not move the activity outside the definition.

### What happens if I am caught volunteering on a tourist visa?
Deportation and a multi-year ban on re-entering Indonesia.
