Bali and Lombok are an hour apart and are not competing for the same traveller, whatever the comparison articles suggest.

## Where Bali wins

**Food.** Not close. Bali has hundreds of good restaurants across every price point. Lombok has a handful.

**Infrastructure.** Hotels at every level, reliable transport, clinics, ATMs, fast internet, English spoken widely.

**Culture.** Balinese Hinduism produces daily ceremonies, thousands of temples and an artistic tradition that is visible everywhere. It is the single most distinctive thing about the island.

**Variety.** Surf, volcanoes, rice terraces, diving, nightlife, yoga, waterfalls, cities and empty coasts, all within two hours.

**Ease.** Everything is arranged in five minutes. That is worth more on a short trip than any scenery.

## Where Lombok wins

**Beaches.** Comprehensively. The south coast bays — Selong Belanak, Mawun, Tanjung Aan — are white sand, clear water and empty, which Bali's mainland beaches are not.

**Space.** A fraction of the visitors, a fraction of the traffic. Lombok in August feels like Bali in February.

**Trekking.** Rinjani is the best multi-day trek in the region and Bali has nothing comparable.

**Price.** Accommodation and food are meaningfully cheaper for the same standard.

**Surf for learning.** Selong Belanak is a better beginner beach than anything in Bali, and the crowds are a tenth of Canggu's.

## The differences that matter practically

**Religion and culture.** Lombok is predominantly Muslim, Bali Hindu. In practice that means dressing more modestly away from the beaches in Lombok, less alcohol availability outside tourist areas, the call to prayer, and a very different feel during Ramadan.

**Medical.** Bali has international-standard private hospitals. Lombok has a hospital in Mataram and clinics elsewhere, and serious cases go to Bali or Singapore.

**Distances.** Lombok's roads are emptier but the island is large: crossing it takes three to four hours.

**Seismic risk.** Both are active zones. Lombok was hit by major earthquakes in 2018 with significant damage and loss of life, and Rinjani closes for activity.

## Who should choose which

**Choose Bali if** it is your first trip to Indonesia, your trip is under ten days, you want restaurants and variety, you are travelling with small children, or you want everything arranged easily.

**Choose Lombok if** you want beaches and space, you are trekking Rinjani, you are learning to surf, you have been to Bali before, or you are on a tight budget.

**Do both if** you have two weeks. Four to five nights in Bali, four in Lombok or the Gilis, with the flight or fast boat between them.

## What about the Gilis

The Gili Islands are administratively part of Lombok and are a third option again: car-free, tiny, turquoise, with excellent cheap diving and no culture to speak of beyond a small local village on each. They are the easiest add-on to either island and the most common compromise.

## The honest summary

Bali is a better destination and Lombok has better beaches. If the beaches are the point of the trip, go to Lombok. If anything else is, go to Bali and take a boat to the Gilis for three nights.

## Two itineraries, compared

**A week in Bali:** three nights Ubud, four nights on the coast, one long day trip. Varied, easy, full of food and culture, and busy.

**A week in Lombok:** four nights Kuta Lombok for the southern bays and surf, three nights in the north for Rinjani's foothills or the Gilis. Emptier, cheaper, more time on beaches, less to do in the evenings.

Both are good weeks. The Bali one has more in it; the Lombok one has more space in it.

## What Lombok does not have

No equivalent of Ubud. No restaurant scene beyond a handful of places in Kuta and Senggigi. Far fewer good hotels at the mid range. Limited nightlife anywhere. And nothing resembling Bali's density of temples, ceremonies and daily visible culture.

If any of those are the reason you are travelling, Lombok is not the answer, and the Gilis are the compromise that gets you the water without giving up the trip.

## Common questions

### Is Lombok cheaper than Bali?
Yes, meaningfully, for accommodation and food at the same standard. Activities cost about the same.

### Is Lombok better than Bali for beaches?
Yes. The south-coast bays are white sand and empty, which Bali's mainland beaches are not.

### Is Lombok worth visiting if you have been to Bali?
Yes, particularly for the south-coast beaches and Rinjani. It offers the space Bali has lost.

### Is Lombok safe for tourists?
Yes, with the usual caution. It is quieter and less used to tourism, and modest dress away from the beach is appreciated.

### Can you do Bali and Lombok in two weeks?
Comfortably: four to five nights in Bali, four in Lombok or the Gilis, and a 30-minute flight or fast boat between them.
