Ubud itself is expensive, crowded and largely developed. The interesting decisions are in the ring of villages around it.

## The options

**Penestanan** sits on the ridge west of Ubud, reached by steps or a narrow road. Long-established foreign community, walkable to Ubud, rice terraces on the plateau. The most convenient of the alternatives and priced accordingly.

**Sayan** overlooks the Ayung river valley to the west. The most dramatic outlook near Ubud and home to the best-known luxury properties. Valley-edge plots carry large premiums and severe building constraints.

**Tegallalang** runs north through the famous terraces. Cheaper, more agricultural, more of a drive, and increasingly built along the main road.

**Southern Gianyar**, toward Sanur, is cheapest and least Ubud-like.

## The constraint that defines the area

Ubud's most beautiful plots are frequently the least buildable.

River valleys create the views and also create enforced setbacks. Green zone agricultural designation is widespread across Gianyar. A gorge-edge plot with a spectacular outlook often means a building envelope pushed well back from the edge.

**Establish the buildable footprint before you value the view.** After setbacks, KDB, KLB and green area requirements, the number is often a fraction of the plot.

## Access is the second constraint

Many plots here are reached by lanes too narrow for construction vehicles. That raises build cost, lengthens timelines and limits guest access afterwards.

Walk the access route before you commit, and ask whether a concrete truck can reach the site.

## The demand pattern

Wellness, retreats, workshops and longer stays. Less seasonal than the coast, with a lower nightly ceiling. Occupancy can be competitive on an annual basis even where rates are not.

## Common questions

### Is Penestanan a good area in Ubud?
It is the most convenient of the surrounding villages, walkable to Ubud with an established foreign community and rice terrace outlooks.

### Why is Sayan expensive?
The Ayung river valley outlook is the most dramatic near Ubud, and valley-edge land is limited and heavily constrained by setbacks.

### Is Tegallalang worth buying in?
Cheaper than Ubud and Penestanan, more agricultural, and a longer drive. Zoning is the main thing to verify since most of it is green zone.

### Can you build on rice terraces in Ubud?
Generally not for tourism accommodation. Green zone agricultural designation is widespread across Gianyar and it is not negotiable.

### Is Ubud good for rental income?
It performs on longer stays, retreats and wellness programming, with steadier occupancy than the coast and a lower nightly ceiling.
