The visa on arrival is how most visitors enter, and the rules around it are simple until people start working on it.

## The basics

| Item | Position |
| --- | --- |
| Duration | 30 days |
| Extension | Once, for a further 30 days |
| Maximum stay | 60 days total |
| Where | On arrival at the airport, or e-VOA online in advance |
| Eligibility | A published list of countries |

The e-VOA applied for online before travel is generally the smoother route, because it lets you skip the payment queue on arrival.

## What it permits

Tourism, family visits, meetings, conferences, and looking at property. Buying property, meeting notaries, walking plots and signing as a buyer all sit comfortably within it. You are spending money, not earning it.

## What it does not permit

Work of any kind. Since the Dharma Dewata task force became operational on 15 April 2026, that has stopped being a theoretical distinction. Roughly 100 officers across Canggu, Ubud, Seminyak, Kerobokan and Uluwatu detained 62 foreign nationals in the first three weeks.

The test being applied is activity, not payment. Remote work for a foreign employer, yoga instruction, wellness workshops, unpaid volunteering and sponsored social posts have all been treated as work. A free villa stay in exchange for promotion counts as compensation.

## Extending

The extension is applied for before the initial 30 days expire, either online or through an agent. Leave time: applying on day 29 is how people end up overstaying because a document was wrong.

Agents charge a modest fee and handle the process. For a single extension it is usually worth it.

## Overstay

Fines accrue daily from the first day. Longer overstays escalate toward detention, deportation and a re-entry ban. It is not a fee you can elect to pay in exchange for staying longer, and treating it that way has become considerably more expensive.

## When you need something else

If you intend to stay beyond 60 days, work remotely, run a business, retire here or join family, the visa on arrival is the wrong instrument. The E33G covers remote work for a foreign employer, and investor, family and retirement routes exist for the others.

## Common questions

### How long can you stay in Bali on a visa on arrival?
Thirty days, extendable once for a further thirty, so sixty days in total.

### Can you extend a Bali visa on arrival twice?
No. One extension only. Beyond sixty days you need a different visa.

### Can I work remotely on a Bali visa on arrival?
No. Remote work is not permitted and is being actively enforced. The E33G is the lawful route for people employed by a foreign company.

### What is the fine for overstaying in Bali?
A daily fine from the first day, escalating for longer overstays toward detention, deportation and a re-entry ban.

### Can I buy property on a tourist visa in Bali?
Viewing, meeting notaries and signing as a buyer are not work. Hak Pakai in your own name requires residency, but leasehold and company structures do not.
