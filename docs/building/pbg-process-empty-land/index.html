How long does it take to get a PBG on empty land in Bali?

Up to six months from bare land to building approval, and the regency you are in changes the answer by months.

Every building in Indonesia needs a PBG, the building approval, before construction legally begins. Everything runs through the national SIMBG system.

From bare land to an issued PBG, plan for up to six months.

The sequence

#StageTypical duration
1Due diligence and preparation1–2 weeks
2PKKPR: spatial suitability confirmation via OSS2–4 weeks
3Land survey and topography1–2 weeks
4Architectural, structural and MEP design3–6 weeks
5Technical document preparation2–3 weeks
6Upload to SIMBG~1 week
7Administrative verification2–3 weeks
8Technical evaluation by the public works office3–6 weeks
9TPT/TPA expert review, if required2–4 weeks
10PBG issued electronically1–2 weeks
11Construction may begin:

Stage 2 is the gate. PKKPR comes before everything, without spatial suitability confirmed, the rest of the sequence has nothing to stand on. If there are document deficiencies at any stage, the file returns to the relevant step rather than continuing.

Your regency decides months of this

RegencyTime to PBGWhy
Denpasar3–5 monthsBest digital integration, mostly residential and small commercial
Tabanan4–5 monthsMany projects in green zones and rice fields need extra approvals
Gianyar4–5 monthsCultural-heritage verification, coordination with cultural agencies
Badung5–6 monthsHighest tourism volume, strict technical evaluation, intensive inspections
Klungkung5–6 monthsSmaller team, longer queues; marine and spatial coordination

Badung is where most people buy and the slowest place to get approved. That is not a coincidence, the volume causes the queue.

What you will be asked for

  • Land certificate, SHM or HGB
  • PKKPR approval
  • Identity and NPWP
  • Site plan
  • Architectural, structural and MEP drawings
  • Technical calculations and reports
  • Environmental document, at the level your project requires
  • Statement letters, including neighbouring-owner acknowledgement

After construction

PBG is not the end. SLF, the certificate of building functionality, must be obtained once construction completes, and it is what makes the building legally usable.

A PBG is valid for a construction period of around two years, extendable by one. Starting late can mean starting again.

What shortens it

Complete, valid documents at first submission. Most of the delay in these timelines is resubmission, not government processing. A file that goes back for deficiencies loses weeks each time.

Respond to revision requests immediately. A file sitting in your inbox is a file not moving.

Nobody can quote you a PBG timeline without knowing the regency, the zone, and whether the site sits in a digitised RDTR area. Anyone who quotes one anyway is guessing.
