Is Asia really cheaper on tax than Europe?

Europe adds a levy most years. Asia opens a door. The part people get wrong is that the tax follows the person, not the property.

By Kai, Bali property adviser Updated 2026-09-13 2 min read

On this page

  1. The direction of travel
  2. What Indonesia actually charges
  3. The mistake that costs people money
  4. What a treaty does and does not do
  5. The honest summary
  6. Common questions

The comparison is real, and almost everyone draws the wrong conclusion from it.

The direction of travel

Europe has been adding: wealth taxes in Norway, Spain and Switzerland, a property wealth tax in France, a deemed-return system in the Netherlands, and the closure of most golden visa routes.

Southeast Asia has been opening: long-stay visas in Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia, no net wealth taxes, and in Indonesia's case no tax on the fact of owning at all.

What Indonesia actually charges

EventCharge
BuyingBuyer transfer duty around 5 percent
HoldingAnnual land and building tax, modest
EarningIncome tax on rental revenue
SellingFinal transfer tax on the sale value
Owning wealthNothing

That is a different shape from a wealth tax. You are taxed when value moves or income arrives, not annually for existing.

The mistake that costs people money

Tax follows residence, not the asset.

If you remain tax resident in Spain, Norway or the Netherlands, buying in Bali does not remove the property from your home country's calculation. Most European wealth taxes reach the worldwide assets of residents, and most income tax systems assess worldwide income.

Moving your money changes nothing. Moving yourself changes everything, and that is a serious decision involving exit taxes, tie-breaker tests and genuinely living somewhere else.

Anyone selling a structure that supposedly delivers the tax outcome while you keep living at home is selling you an investigation.

What a treaty does and does not do

Indonesia has treaties with most countries foreign buyers come from. They allocate taxing rights and relieve double taxation, usually by credit.

They do not remove the Indonesian obligation on Indonesian-source income, and relief is not automatic. It requires a certificate of residence filed in the right form.

The honest summary

Asia is genuinely cheaper on tax for people who actually move. For people who stay put and buy remotely, the saving is much smaller than the marketing implies, and the compliance burden is larger.

Common questions

Is Indonesia a tax haven?

No. It taxes income and transactions normally. What it does not have is a net wealth tax, which is the specific difference against several European countries.

Do I stop paying European tax if I buy in Bali?

No. Wealth and income taxes generally follow tax residence and reach worldwide assets and income. Only changing residence changes that.

What tax do I pay on Bali rental income?

Indonesian income tax as local-source income, at a rate that depends on whether you are taxed as a non-resident, a resident, or through a company.

How does a double tax treaty help?

It allocates taxing rights and relieves double taxation, usually through a credit at home. Relief must be claimed with a residence certificate.

Is it worth moving tax residence for this?

Only if you genuinely intend to live somewhere else. Exit taxes, tie-breaker tests and substance requirements make half-measures expensive.
