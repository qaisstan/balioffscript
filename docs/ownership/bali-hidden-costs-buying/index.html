What are the hidden costs of buying in Bali?

Budget the purchase price and nothing else and you are roughly 7 percent short before you have furnished anything.

By Kai, Bali property adviser Updated 2026-09-06 3 min read

The price is the headline. These are the costs that turn up afterwards, and they are the reason deals collapse at the deed stage.

At the transaction

CostTypical
Buyer transfer duty on a titled transferAround 5 percent
Notary and PPAT fees1 to 2.5 percent, negotiable
Independent legal reviewUSD 1,000 to 5,000
Currency spread on transferOften more than the legal fee
TotalBudget around 7 to 8 percent

The currency spread is the one nobody counts. On a large transfer, a high street bank's margin can exceed everything you paid your lawyer.

Setting up

  • PT PMA formation and paid-up capital, where the structure requires a company
  • Title conversion, where land moves from SHM to SHGB or SHP
  • Utility connections, which on remote plots can be substantial
  • Electricity capacity upgrade, common on older properties
  • Water, particularly a well and its permits, or a storage tank

Getting it usable

  • Furniture and fit-out, USD 25,000 to 45,000 for a three-bedroom villa at rental standard
  • Immediate repairs, which a survey should have priced before you bought
  • Pool servicing and plant, frequently overdue on older stock
  • Photography and listing setup, if it will rent

Then, every year

  • Annual land and building tax
  • Insurance, building, contents and public liability
  • Staff, plus BPJS at roughly 14 percent and THR of one month's salary annually
  • PT PMA compliance: accounting, tax filings, LKPM reporting, financial statements
  • Sinking fund for the five-year refurbishment
  • Utilities in void periods, which do not stop when guests do

The two most commonly missed

The sinking fund. Tropical wear means a full refurbishment cycle roughly every five years. Owners who do not provision take it in one year and that year reads as a loss.

PT PMA running costs. These accrue whether or not the company trades. A structure modelled without them is a structure modelled as non-compliant.

What this does to your yield

Add 7 to 8 percent to the purchase price at entry, then a realistic annual cost base, and the net return moves a long way from the gross figure in the sales deck.

That is not an argument against buying. It is an argument for doing the arithmetic before you commit rather than discovering it in year two.

Common questions

What are the closing costs when buying property in Bali?

Around 7 to 8 percent all in: roughly 5 percent buyer transfer duty on a titled transfer, 1 to 2.5 percent notary and PPAT fees, plus independent legal review.

Who pays the transfer tax in Bali?

The buyer normally bears the acquisition duty and the seller a final tax on disposal. Who actually pays what is negotiable and should be explicit in the deed.

What ongoing costs does a Bali villa have?

Annual land and building tax, insurance, staff with BPJS and THR, utilities, maintenance, company compliance if a PT PMA holds it, and a sinking fund for refurbishment.

Is legal review worth the cost in Bali?

It is the cheapest line in the transaction and the only one that exists purely to protect you. It is also the one buyers most often cut.

How much should I budget above the purchase price?

Roughly 7 to 8 percent for the transaction, plus fit-out and any immediate repairs, plus an annual operating base including a refurbishment provision.
