Ownership45

What foreigners can actually own, and how the three legal routes really work.

  • Can a foreigner own property in Bali?Not freehold. But three legal structures exist, and one of them puts a certificate in your own name.
  • Leasehold or freehold in Bali?Foreigners cannot hold freehold at all, so the real comparison is between leasehold, Hak Pakai and a company holding HGB. Here is how the three actually differ.
  • What makes a Bali leasehold extension clause actually safe?The initial term is not the asset. The extension clause is, and most of them are worthless.
  • What are the property laws in Bali?One national framework, one regional layer, and six rules that decide everything a foreign buyer can and cannot do.
  • What is the difference between SHM, SHGB and Hak Pakai?Four land rights, four different answers to who can hold them and for how long. Getting this wrong is how people end up with nothing.
  • Guaranteed or priority extension in a Bali lease?Two Indonesian phrases that look interchangeable in a lease. One gives you an extension. The other gives you the right to ask.
  • HGB or leasehold: which is actually better?One is a registered right you can mortgage. The other is a contract that expires. The right answer depends on what you intend to do with it.
  • What actually happens if you buy through a nominee?The agreement is void the day it is signed, and since February 2026 it is criminal in Bali.
  • How does Hak Pakai work, and who can actually get it?The one route that registers land in a foreigner's own name. Requires residency, and most people are never told it exists.
  • What are the actual steps to buy property in Bali?Five stages, and the money should not move until the second one. Where the deposit sits decides whether you get it back.
  • Is a Bali leasehold registered anywhere?Hak Sewa appears on no certificate and in no government register. Your entire protection is the notarised deed, which changes how you verify everything.
  • What happens when your Indonesian land right expires?Every land right in Indonesia is temporary except freehold. On expiry it reverts, and renewal is approval, not entitlement.
  • Can a Bali lease run 80 years?Indonesian practice allows leasehold arrangements reaching about 80 years, but almost no notary will write it as one term. Here is how it is actually built.
  • How does a foreigner actually buy land in Bali?The full sequence, in the order it has to happen, and where the money should be at each step.
  • Who owns the villa you built on leased land?Land and building are separable in Indonesian law. Whether your villa reverts with the land at expiry depends entirely on a clause most leases leave silent.
  • How safe is your money in a Bali property deal?Safer than the horror stories suggest, and only if you do four specific things.
  • Can you sell or transfer a Bali leasehold?Your exit depends on a clause most buyers never read. If assignment needs the landowner's consent and that consent has a price, you do not fully control your own sale.
  • Do you need a prenup to own property in a mixed marriage?Without one, a foreign spouse's interest can contaminate an Indonesian spouse's freehold. This is the most consequential document in a mixed marriage here.
  • What happens to your Bali property when you die?A home country will does not settle an Indonesian land title, and heirs who cannot lawfully hold it face a disposal deadline.
  • How do you sell a property in Bali?Exit is the part nobody plans. Your buyer pool, your remaining lease term and your licence position decide the price far more than the villa does.
  • What are the common property scams in Bali?The losses here follow a small number of repeating patterns, and almost all of them happen before the deed is ever signed.
  • What does a notary do in a Bali property purchase?The PPAT executes the deed and registers the transfer. Using the seller's notary is the cheapest way to lose your only independent check.
  • What should you ask a Bali property agent?Most agents will answer what you ask and not much more. These are the questions that surface the problems before you pay a deposit.
  • Can you get a mortgage in Bali?Foreign buyers here are effectively cash buyers. Local lending is largely closed and the alternatives carry their own problems.
  • What are the hidden costs of buying in Bali?Budget the purchase price and nothing else and you are roughly 7 percent short before you have furnished anything.
  • How does a power of attorney work in Indonesia?Useful for transacting from abroad, and routinely misused as the paperwork propping up an unlawful nominee arrangement.
  • What happens if a Bali property deal goes wrong?Litigation here is slow, expensive and uncertain for foreigners. Almost everything worth doing about a dispute happens before you sign.
  • Can you own a beach in Indonesia?Nobody owns the beach. Understanding what those beachfront businesses actually hold explains a lot about what beachfront land is worth.
  • How does leasehold work in Bali?The instrument most foreign buyers here actually hold. It is a contract, not a title, and that distinction decides what happens when things go wrong.
  • What do all the Bali property terms mean?Every Indonesian term a foreign buyer will meet, in plain English, with what it actually means for you.
  • How do you pay for a property in Bali?Bank transfer, in your own name, to the registered owner, against written conditions. Every alternative exists to make somebody else's life easier.
  • How long does due diligence take in Bali?A clean title takes one to three weeks to verify properly. Anyone pressuring you inside that window is telling you something about the deal.
  • How do you convert a land title in Indonesia?Moving land from a citizen's freehold to a right a foreigner or a company can hold is a normal procedure, and the gap between agreeing it and completing it is where money gets stuck.
  • What is splittable land in Bali?Whether a parcel can be legally divided decides your exit options, your financing and often the price. Most buyers never ask.
  • What documents do you need to buy property in Bali?The list is short, and the ones that matter most are the documents the seller has to produce, not the ones you do.
  • Should you list a Bali villa exclusively or with every agent?Open listings feel safer and usually sell for less. The reason is what an agent will and will not spend money on.
  • How do you prepare a Bali villa for sale?The paperwork takes longer than the presentation and matters more. Start it three months before you list.
  • How do you buy Bali property with a partner?Two people, one lease, and no registered title to record who owns what. The agreement between you is the only thing that decides it.
  • How do joint ventures with Bali landowners work?The landowner contributes land, you contribute capital, and you split the return. It works when the documents are right and fails completely when they are not.
  • What is girik land in Bali?Girik is not a certificate. It is evidence of possession on unregistered land, and it trades at a discount that reflects real risk.
  • What is the risk of buying inherited land in Bali?Land that passed down a family without being formally divided has more than one owner, and the seller may be only one of them.
  • How do you check access rights to Bali land?A road you drove down to view the land may belong to a neighbour. If your access is not documented, it can close.
  • Do you need a land survey in Bali?The advertised area, the certificate area and what is physically fenced are frequently three different numbers. A survey is how you find out.
  • How do off-market property deals work in Bali?The best parcels frequently never reach a listing. Getting access means relationships, and it also means fewer checks have been done for you.
  • Can you buy a Bali villa with a tenant in it?Existing agreements bind you. A tenancy, a management contract or forward bookings all transfer with the property whether you wanted them or not.

Visas35

Every stay permit that matters, with the real requirements and costs.

  • Which KITAS do you need for Bali?KITAS is not one thing. There are several, they permit different activities, and choosing by duration rather than activity is how people get deported.
  • What counts as illegal work on a Bali tourist visa?A dedicated immigration task force detained 62 foreigners in its first three weeks. Sponsored posts and unpaid volunteering both count.
  • Which Indonesian visa do you actually need?The index codes changed. Here is what each one lets you do, and which ones unlock property ownership.
  • What are the real requirements for the E33G remote worker KITAS?One year of residency for remote workers, with an income floor and one rule people break immediately.
  • How does the Investor KITAS (E28A) work?Two years of residency attached to your PT PMA shareholding, and the permit most property-holding structures run on.
  • What does the Second Home Visa actually require?A deposit in an Indonesian state bank or qualifying property, in exchange for five or ten years of residence with no sponsor.
  • How do you get permanent residence in Indonesia?ITAP is converted from a qualifying temporary permit, not applied for directly. Which permit you hold decides whether the door exists.
  • What is the Indonesian Golden Visa, and is it worth it?A long-stay route for investors who commit at defined levels. It buys time and simplicity, not a single property right a cheaper KITAS would not also unlock.
  • How does a spouse or family KITAS work?Five different sub-indices depending on exactly who you are joining. Choosing the wrong one is the usual delay.
  • Which visa lets you come and check out a property properly?There is a purpose-built pre-investment category. Most buyers use a tourist visa and run out of time.
  • Why did all the Indonesian visa codes change?The system went from 133 indices to 110 in 2025, and employer-sponsored work visas from 31 to 6. Old advice is now wrong.
  • Can you retire in Bali, and what does it take?There is a route built for it. The requirements are financial rather than age-based, and property gives you nothing.
  • Can you legally work remotely from Bali?Yes, and there is a specific permit for it. Most people arrive on the wrong one.
  • How does the Bali visa on arrival work?Thirty days, extendable once for another thirty. It permits tourism and meetings, and nothing that counts as work.
  • What happens if you overstay in Indonesia?A daily fine from day one, and past a certain point it stops being a fine and becomes detention and a re-entry ban.
  • How does the Indonesian spouse visa work?Marriage to an Indonesian citizen opens a residence route, and eventually KITAP. It does not by itself grant the right to work.
  • What visa do you need for business meetings in Indonesia?It covers meetings, negotiations and site visits. It does not cover doing the work, and that line is being enforced.
  • What is an EPO and do you need one?Ending a KITAS is a process, not a departure. Leaving without closing it properly causes problems on your next application.
  • What is the Indonesian business visit visa?Meetings, negotiations and site inspections for a foreign company. Not delivering work, and that line is being enforced.
  • What is the pre-investment visa?Built for people assessing whether to invest here, which is exactly the position most foreign property buyers are in before they form a company.
  • Can you volunteer legally in Indonesia?Unpaid work is still work under the enforcement test being applied. There is a category for volunteering, and people who skip it are the ones being detained.
  • How do you extend a KITAS?Extensions are not automatic and the deadlines are unforgiving. The bigger risk is that a lapse resets your path to permanent stay.
  • How do you extend a visa on arrival?Thirty days extendable once, done online or through an agent. Apply on day 29 and a single rejected document becomes an overstay.
  • How do you extend a retirement KITAS?Extension depends on the qualifying conditions still being met, and the one that changes with age is insurance rather than income.
  • Can you get permanent stay through employment?Consecutive qualifying years on a work KITAS open the door to permanent stay. Continuity is the whole requirement and it is easy to break.
  • Can an investor get permanent stay in Indonesia?The route exists, and it rests entirely on the PT PMA staying compliant for years. Your residence is only as good as your filings.
  • What permit does a child of an Indonesian citizen need?Indonesia allows limited dual citizenship for children of mixed marriages, with a choice that must be made by a deadline most families miss.
  • What is SKTT and do you need a domicile letter?Getting the KITAS is not the end of the process. Local registration is a separate step and people only discover it when something else is blocked.
  • When do you need a sworn translation?Foreign documents generally need legalisation and a sworn translation before Indonesian offices will accept them, and the chain takes weeks.
  • Can you study in Indonesia on a student visa?Sponsored by the institution, not by you, and it does not permit work. The language-school route is the one foreigners in Bali actually use.
  • How does a multiple entry visa work?Two different numbers govern it, and confusing them is how people overstay without ever intending to.
  • What happens if your visa expires while applying for another?Switching categories leaves a window where one permit has ended and the next has not arrived. That gap is the risk.
  • What is the Indonesian silver hair visa?A longer-stay route for older applicants meeting financial conditions, positioned between the retirement KITAS and the golden visa.
  • What visa can a former Indonesian citizen get?Indonesia recognises a connection to former citizens and their descendants, with routes that are more accessible than the general ones.
  • What is the Indonesia global talent golden visa?A residency route based on qualifications and reputation rather than a property purchase or an employer sponsor.

Companies16

PT PMA setup, capital rules, licensing and the annual compliance load.

  • How much capital do you need for a PT PMA in 2026?The paid-up requirement dropped 75% in October 2025. The investment plan requirement did not.
  • What does a PT PMA actually cost to keep running each year?Setup gets quoted. The annual load rarely does, and it is where these structures quietly fail.
  • Can a PT PMA still get a villa rental licence in Bali?No. Bali closed the villa and homestay classifications to new foreign-owned companies in July 2026. Two routes remain open.
  • How long does it really take to set up a PT PMA?Six to ten weeks to a trading licence if nothing stalls. Here is the phase-by-phase reality, and what usually goes wrong.
  • How do you hire staff in a PT PMA?Base salaries are low and the obligations around them are not. Termination in particular is far harder and more expensive than foreign owners expect.
  • What is an NIB and how does OSS work?The NIB is your business identity number and OSS is the system that issues it. Getting the classification right is the whole job.
  • How do you close a PT PMA?A dormant company does not quietly disappear. Obligations keep accruing and the eventual cost of regularising exceeds maintaining it.
  • How do you open a company bank account in Indonesia?The step that stalls more company formations than any other, and the one you cannot do remotely.
  • What accounting does a PT PMA need?Monthly filings, quarterly reports and annual statements, owed whether or not the company earned anything.
  • Can a foreigner be a director of an Indonesian company?Holding shares is not the same as being allowed to work. Directors without the right authorisation are exposed, and enforcement has tightened.
  • Can a PT PMA use a virtual office?Cheap and widely used for company formation, and the point where banks and licensing sometimes say no.
  • What is a representative office in Indonesia?A foreign company can have a legal presence here without forming a PT PMA. It cannot earn revenue, which is the entire trade-off.
  • What is a yayasan and can a foreigner run one?The non-profit vehicle behind most schools, clinics and NGOs here. It cannot distribute profit, and using one to hold a business is a known trap.
  • PT PMA or Hak Pakai for a Bali property?One is a personal right that needs residency. The other is a company that needs a business. Choosing on cost alone is how people end up with the wrong one.
  • What is a RUPS and does your PT PMA need one?An Indonesian company law requirement that single-shareholder companies routinely forget, and that surfaces when you try to sell or restructure.
  • What is a PMDN and can a foreigner use one?A PMDN is a domestic investment company with no foreign shareholding. Using one to hold property for a foreigner is a nominee arrangement.

Tax16

Transaction taxes, rental income tax, NPWP and what actually gets enforced.

  • What taxes do you pay when buying property in Bali?Around 5% for the buyer on a titled transfer, and zero on a leasehold, which is a bigger deal than it sounds.
  • What tax do you owe on villa rental income in Bali?10% final for a resident individual, 20% for a non-resident, corporate rates inside a PT PMA. Plus a 10% regional tax the platforms do not collect for you.
  • When do you become an Indonesian tax resident, and do you need an NPWP?183 days, and then Indonesia taxes your worldwide income. Most long-stay foreigners have not done this arithmetic.
  • How do you move money to Indonesia to buy property?Bank transfer, documented, traceable. Never cash, and never through someone else's account.
  • Will you be taxed twice on Indonesian income?Indonesia has treaties with most countries foreign buyers come from. They allocate taxing rights; they do not make the income invisible.
  • What is BPHTB and who pays it?The buyer's acquisition duty on a titled transfer, around 5 percent, calculated on whichever value the tax office decides is higher.
  • What is the annual property tax in Bali?Modest by European or Australian standards, based on the government's assessed value, and the receipts matter more than the amount.
  • What tax do you pay when selling property in Indonesia?Indonesia does not tax the gain. It taxes the sale value, which is a very different calculation and usually simpler than people expect.
  • Do you pay tax on Airbnb income in Bali?Platform income is Indonesian-source income regardless of which country the guest paid from, and traceability has improved sharply.
  • How do you get an NPWP in Indonesia?The taxpayer number that most property transactions, company obligations and filings run through. Straightforward to obtain and awkward to be without.
  • Do Australians pay tax twice on Bali property?Indonesia taxes the property income first. Australia assesses your worldwide income and gives credit. Relief is not automatic.
  • What are NITKU and Coretax, and do property buyers need them?Indonesia rebuilt its tax administration in 2025 and 2026. If your tax number is not linked correctly, filings and transfers stall.
  • Can foreigners get a mortgage in Indonesia?Domestic mortgage lending to foreigners barely exists in practice, and leasehold is not mortgageable at all. Almost everyone pays cash or borrows at home.
  • What is NPWPD and do you need one?The regency-level tax on accommodation that Airbnb does not collect for you, and the registration number that goes with it.
  • Do UK residents pay tax twice on Bali property?Indonesia taxes the property income first. HMRC assesses your worldwide income and gives credit. The balance usually falls in the UK.
  • How does US tax work on Bali property?The United States taxes citizens on worldwide income wherever they live, and the reporting obligations on foreign assets are the part that catches people.

Building & Zoning27

Zone colours, permits, the moratorium, and what it costs to build.

  • What do Bali's zoning colours mean, and how do you check properly?Pink lets you rent. Yellow does not. Green gets demolished. Check it yourself before you pay anything.
  • How much does it cost to build a villa in Bali?Quotes run from IDR 6M to 15M per square metre. The spread is specification, and the number you are shown usually excludes the things that break the budget.
  • What happens if you buy a villa built without a PBG?No licence, no legal rental, and exposure to sealing or demolition. It is more common than the market admits.
  • How much does a swimming pool cost in Bali?A standard villa pool runs USD 15,000 to 25,000 to build. The part owners underestimate is what it costs every month after that.
  • What are KDB and KLB, and why do they decide your project?Zoning tells you if you can build. KDB and KLB tell you how much. A plot can be perfectly legal and still economically pointless.
  • What are the real risks of buying off-plan in Bali?You are paying for something that does not exist yet, on permits that may not exist either. Here is what to verify before the first instalment.
  • How long does it take to get a PBG on empty land in Bali?Up to six months from bare land to building approval, and the regency you are in changes the answer by months.
  • Can you legalise a Bali villa that was built without permits?Often yes, through retroactive PBG and SLF. It takes eight months to a year and it is not guaranteed.
  • What is it really like to build a villa in Bali?The permit sequence, the timeline by regency, and the parts of the process people do not budget for.
  • Where does a Bali villa get its water?Piped supply does not reach everywhere, wells need permits, and on the Bukit water is a genuine operating cost rather than a detail.
  • Should you renovate a villa in Bali?Older stock is cheap for a reason. Renovation can be the best value in the market or a way to buy someone else's permit problem.
  • What does it cost to furnish a villa in Bali?Furniture is cheap and good here. What owners get wrong is how fast it wears out and how rarely it is included in a build quote.
  • Is solar worth it for a Bali villa?The sunshine is excellent and the electricity bill is large, which makes the arithmetic better here than in most places owners come from.
  • How do you choose a developer in Bali?Renders are free. What separates a developer worth paying is what they have finished, who they finished it for, and what they hold if it goes wrong.
  • Should you buy off-plan or completed in Bali?Off-plan promises a discount and delivers construction risk, counterparty risk and a building nobody has inspected. Completed costs more and exists.
  • What should you check at villa handover?The last moment you have leverage is before you accept the building. After that, every defect is a favour you are asking for.
  • What permits do you need to build on leased land in Bali?The permit is applied for in the landowner's name in most cases, which means your right to build has to be written into the lease before you sign it.
  • What are the building rules in Bali?Height limits, setbacks, coverage ratios and local custom all constrain what you can build, and the customary rules are not written in the spatial plan.
  • Do you need an architect to build in Bali?The design decides your build cost, your permit and your nightly rate. It is the cheapest part of the project and the one people cut.
  • How do you deal with termites and pests in a Bali villa?Termites destroy timber structures here faster than owners expect, and treatment before building costs a fraction of repair afterwards.
  • What does it cost to build on a slope in Bali?Sloped land is cheaper per are and more expensive to build on. Retaining, foundations and access frequently cost more than the land discount saved.
  • How should you contract a builder in Bali?There is no licensing regime and no statutory warranty. Whatever protection you have is in the contract you signed and the money you have not yet paid.
  • How does wastewater work in a Bali villa?There is no mains sewer across most of Bali. Every villa treats its own wastewater, and a failed system is the worst problem a rental property can have.
  • How should you specify aircon for a Bali villa?Aircon is the largest running cost and the most common guest complaint. Both are specification problems rather than operating problems.
  • What does landscaping a Bali villa cost?The garden is a large part of what a Bali villa sells on, it is routinely omitted from build quotes, and it is the cheapest thing that changes a nightly rate.
  • Does a Bali villa need backup power?Outages happen, more in the wet season and more in some areas. For a property with paying guests, four hours without power is a review.
  • Should you build in bamboo in Bali?Bamboo buildings are spectacular, genuinely sustainable and considerably more demanding to own than the photographs suggest.

  • How do you protect a Bali villa from guest damage?Most damage is minor and cumulative rather than dramatic. The protection is screening, a deposit, and a written record of condition.
  • Can you host weddings and events at a Bali villa?Events pay several times a normal night and carry licensing, neighbour and community consequences that a nightly booking does not.
  • Does land banking work in Bali?Buying land and waiting works when the land appreciates faster than a lease term decays. On a leasehold, it usually does not.
  • Which platforms should a Bali villa list on?More listings means more exposure and one calendar to keep in sync. Getting that wrong in August is the most expensive administrative error available.

Areas40

Land prices, demand and constraints, area by area.

  • Where should you actually buy in Bali?Canggu for liquidity, Uluwatu for upside, Sanur and Ubud for value. What each area costs and what it costs you.
  • What does land actually cost in Bali right now?Area by area, in dollars per are, with the reason each one is priced the way it is.
  • Is Canggu still worth buying into?The most liquid market in Bali, and the most saturated. Badung enforcement is the tightest on the island.
  • Should you buy in Pererenan instead of Canggu?Where Canggu money moved when Canggu got expensive. Same Badung rules, more green zone, more risk of buying something you cannot build on.
  • What is the real risk of buying in Uluwatu?The strongest upside case in Bali, and the place that demolished buildings in 2025. Setbacks and pink zoning are everything here.
  • Is Sanur the best value in Bali right now?Established, calmer, cheaper per square metre, and the fastest regency in Bali for permits.
  • What should you know before buying in Ubud?Strong occupancy and a different guest profile. Gianyar's constraint is sacred-site setbacks and very small buildable ratios.
  • Is Tabanan the next Canggu?The clearest appreciation story in Bali and the hardest place to get a commercial accommodation project approved.
  • Is Lombok a better bet than Bali right now?Cheaper land, and, crucially, Bali's 2026 restrictions do not apply there. Different province, different rules.
  • What should you know about buying on Nusa Penida?Bali's rules apply in full, permits take longest of any regency, and infrastructure is the constraint nobody prices.
  • Is Seminyak still worth buying in?The most established foreign market on the island. Highest density, highest prices, almost no land, and the deepest resale pool.
  • Is Jimbaran a good place to buy?Closest established area to the airport, calmer than the west coast, and priced below both Seminyak and the Bukit clifftops.
  • Is North Bali worth buying in?The cheapest coastal land on the island, black sand beaches, and a rental market that has not arrived. A long-horizon bet.
  • What is an are of land in Bali?Land here is priced per are, which is 100 square metres. Confusing it with a square metre or an acre is an expensive arithmetic error.
  • Can you buy property on the Gili Islands?Three small islands with real tourism demand, no cars, limited fresh water, and a land market that rewards caution over enthusiasm.
  • Is East Bali worth buying in?Rice terraces, Mount Agung, almost no tourism infrastructure, and prices a fraction of the south. A lifestyle buy more than a yield play.
  • Where should you live in Bali if you surf?The swell window decides which coast works when, and that should decide where you buy far more than the marketing does.
  • Which areas of Bali are best for investment?The areas with the best rental demand and the areas with the best entry price are not the same, and the gap between them is the whole decision.
  • Is Umalas a good place to buy in Bali?Sandwiched between Canggu and Seminyak, quieter than both, with rice fields still in the gaps. The area families pick when Canggu gets loud.
  • Is Kerobokan worth buying in?Between Seminyak and Canggu, with real local infrastructure and prices that reflect the absence of a beach.
  • Is Nusa Dua a good investment?Gated, manicured, hotel-dominated and unlike anywhere else in Bali. The most controlled property environment on the island.
  • Can you still buy in Bingin?One of the most sought cliff positions on the Bukit, and the area where enforcement against unpermitted cliff structures has bitten hardest.
  • Is Nusa Lembongan worth buying?Thirty minutes from Sanur by boat, genuinely beautiful, and constrained by water, power and a resale market of almost nobody.
  • Is Seseh the next Pererenan?Black sand, a golf course, temples on the beach and a wave of construction arriving from the south. Early, but no longer secret.
  • Is Batu Bolong worth the premium?The most walkable stretch in Canggu and the most expensive per are. Walkability is the whole product and it is genuinely scarce.
  • Is Kedungu the next investment corridor?Where the west coast development wave meets Tabanan's cheap land. Real surf, real construction, and demand that has not caught up.
  • Is Pecatu or Ungasan better value than Uluwatu?The interior Bukit, minutes from the famous breaks, at a fraction of clifftop prices. The trade is the view and the water.
  • Where should you buy around Ubud?Central Ubud is congested and built out. The value and the views are in the villages around it, and each one behaves differently.
  • Is East Bali coast worth buying?Black sand, world-class diving and prices a fraction of the south. Rental demand is thin and seasonal, and that is the whole trade.
  • Is Echo Beach a good place to buy?The northern end of Canggu, surf-led rather than cafe-led, slightly cheaper and with a different guest entirely.
  • Is Balangan or Padang Padang worth buying?Two of the best beaches on the Bukit, both constrained by access, water and the setback rules that have already bitten nearby.
  • Is Munduk worth buying in?Waterfalls, coffee plantations, cool air and almost no rental market. A lifestyle purchase that should never be underwritten on yield.
  • Is Kuta or Legian still worth buying?The original tourist strip, now the cheapest developed coastline in the south. Unfashionable is not the same as unprofitable.
  • Should you buy in Denpasar?The capital, almost entirely a local market, and the place to look if you want tenants who live here rather than visit.
  • Should you buy on Nusa Ceningan?Connected to Lembongan by a yellow bridge and smaller than anywhere else foreigners buy. Every constraint on Lembongan applies harder here.
  • What does beachfront land cost in Bali?The price is high, the supply is nearly fixed, and the setback rules mean a significant part of what you buy cannot be built on.
  • Is Berawa a good place to buy property?Berawa has the highest occupancy in Canggu and the highest land prices. Whether it works depends entirely on what term you can get.
  • What is property like in Petitenget and Batu Belig?The strip between Seminyak and Canggu has the amenity of both and the traffic of neither. It is the most underrated position in the south.
  • Is Tanah Lot a good place to buy?A famous temple, a championship golf course, black sand coast and land at a fraction of Canggu. The demand has not arrived at the same speed.
  • Is Munggu or Tumbak Bayuh worth buying?The rice fields immediately inland from the Pererenan corridor, cheaper than the coast and close enough to use it.

Living Here34

Whether Bali works as a place to live, and where the market goes next.

  • Is Bali actually a good place to live?For some people, genuinely. The honest answer depends on things nobody puts in the brochure.
  • Should you move to Bali?Ask what you will actually do here first. The visa follows the activity, and most disappointment starts with that order reversed.
  • Is Bali becoming the next Singapore or Dubai?The capital is arriving. The legal framework it usually requires is not, and that gap is the whole story.
  • What does it actually cost to live in Bali?What drives the number, what people underestimate, and why the figures you see online are usually somebody else's life.
  • Where should you actually live in Bali?Where to live and where to invest are different questions with different answers.
  • Bali, Thailand or Portugal for property?Only one of the three lets a foreigner own anything outright. That is the decision, not the yield.
  • How much does it cost to rent in Bali?One-bedroom villas start around IDR 17.5 million a month in Canggu. The bigger shock is that most landlords want a year up front.
  • Is Bali safe?Violent crime is not the risk. The roads are, by a wide margin, and almost nobody prepares for that.
  • What is healthcare in Bali actually like?Routine care is fine and affordable. Serious care is where people get financially destroyed, and the number is bigger than you think.
  • What do international schools in Bali cost?From around USD 3,000 to over USD 30,000 a year. And the headline tuition is not the number you actually pay.
  • What is it actually like to work remotely from Bali?The internet is better than the reputation. The legal position is worse than most people assume.
  • Do you need a licence to drive a scooter in Bali?Yes. And the reason it matters is not the police fine, it is what happens to your insurance claim.
  • What is Bali actually like in rainy season?Roughly November to March. It is not the constant downpour people fear, and it is not the postcard either.
  • What is it like moving to Bali with children?Families live in a completely different Bali to everyone else, and it costs roughly three times as much.
  • Can a foreigner open a bank account in Indonesia?With a KITAS it is straightforward. Without one it is mostly not possible, and the workarounds sold to tourists carry real risk.
  • Can you bring a pet to Bali?Bali is a rabies control zone, and the honest answer is that bringing a dog or cat in is restricted and difficult, not simply expensive.
  • Should you ship your belongings to Bali?Sea freight takes months and attracts duty. For most people the honest answer is to bring two suitcases and buy the rest here.
  • How much is electricity in Bali?Air conditioning and the pool pump are the bill. A three-bedroom villa running hard costs far more than owners budget from a residential baseline.
  • Is the internet good in Bali?Fibre in the main expat areas is genuinely fine for remote work. Outside them it is not, and the gap is sharper than people expect.
  • How do you get an Indonesian driving licence?An IDP endorsed for motorcycles covers a visit. If you live here, the local SIM removes the insurance argument entirely.
  • When is the best time to visit Bali?Dry season is easier and busier. Wet season is cheaper, quieter, and the only honest time to inspect a property you intend to buy.
  • How bad is Bali traffic?Distances here are measured in minutes, not kilometres, and the two diverge badly. It should change where you buy.
  • How risky are earthquakes and volcanoes in Bali?Bali sits on an active margin. It rarely affects daily life and it should affect how you build, insure and choose a location.
  • Do you need to speak Indonesian in Bali?You can live here on English alone. You will pay more, understand less, and stay outside most of what is actually happening.
  • Where do you work from in Bali?Canggu has the density, Ubud the quiet, Sanur the people who actually want to work. All three beat a villa connection you cannot rely on.
  • What should you know about Balinese culture?Ceremony is not a tourist attraction here, it is the calendar. Understanding that changes how you build, hire and live.
  • How bad are Bali's water and waste problems?Groundwater depletion and waste management are the island's real constraints, and both are becoming regulatory risks for property owners.
  • What is moving to Bali actually like?The first three months are the holiday. Months four to twelve are the ones that decide whether you stay.
  • What health insurance do you need in Bali?Good private care exists in the south, and serious cases leave the island. Evacuation cover is the difference between an inconvenience and a catastrophe.
  • How do you send money out of Indonesia?Transfers out attract more scrutiny than transfers in. The constraint is documentation rather than permission, and planning it saves both time and spread.
  • What is it like having a baby in Bali?Good private maternity care exists in the south. The paperwork after the birth is where foreign parents get caught.
  • Can a foreigner buy a car in Indonesia?Registration needs a KITAS or a local arrangement, and the arrangement people use to avoid that is the same pattern that fails elsewhere.
  • Where do you buy furniture in Bali?Locally made solid timber costs a fraction of imported equivalents and suits the climate better. Knowing where to go is most of it.
  • How does childcare work in Bali?Domestic help is affordable in a way it is not at home, and employing someone carries the full Indonesian labour framework whether you realise it or not.

Bali vs The World23

What foreigners can own, pay and keep in Bali against Dubai, Thailand, Portugal, Japan and the rest.

  • Which countries still sell residency for property?Spain closed its golden visa. Portugal removed the property route. The list of countries still trading residency for capital is shorter every year.
  • Where in Asia can foreigners actually own property?Almost nowhere lets a foreigner own land. What separates these markets is what you get instead, and how long you keep it.
  • Which countries tax you for being rich?Norway, Spain and Switzerland tax what you own. Indonesia taxes what you earn. Over a long hold that difference compounds into real money.
  • How does the Dutch Box 3 tax work?The Dutch system taxes an assumed return on your assets rather than what they actually earned, which is why it has been fought through the courts.
  • Dubai or Bali for property?Dubai gives you freehold and no income tax. Bali gives you 30 years and no freehold at all. The money keeps arriving in both.
  • Thailand or Bali for property?Thailand gives you a condominium freehold and prosecutes the company workaround. Indonesia gives you no land at all and has criminalised the equivalent.
  • Why are so many Australians buying in Bali?Australians are the largest single group of foreign buyers here, and they keep making the same three mistakes.
  • Is Bali running out of land?Bali is building faster than almost anywhere in Asia and running out of the land worth building on at the same time. Both are true.
  • Can foreigners own property in Japan?Japan lets any foreigner own land outright with no restriction. Bali lets you own none of it. The money still goes to Bali, and the reason is instructive.
  • Can foreigners buy property in Vietnam?Vietnam grants foreigners 50 years on an apartment. Indonesia grants 30 on a lease. Only one of them has a defined renewal path.
  • Is Malaysia MM2H better than Bali?Malaysia lets foreigners own freehold above state minimums and ties a long-stay visa to it. Bali offers neither. The trade-off is demand.
  • Can foreigners buy property in the Philippines?Foreigners can hold up to 40 percent of a condominium building and none of the land. Indonesia allows none of either, and still draws more capital.
  • Is Asia really cheaper on tax than Europe?Europe adds a levy most years. Asia opens a door. The part people get wrong is that the tax follows the person, not the property.
  • What does $130,000 buy in Bali?It is the number attached to Indonesia's Second Home route, and it buys very different things depending on where you point it.
  • Is it cheaper to build in Bali than Australia?Over 1,500 Australian builders collapsed in a year and buyers lost deposits. Building here costs a fraction, and the risks are entirely different ones.
  • Can foreigners buy property in Jakarta?The same national rules apply, but the market is apartments, tenants are corporate, and nobody is buying it for the lifestyle.
  • Is Sumba or Labuan Bajo the next Bali?Spectacular, genuinely early, and separated from Bali by a decade of infrastructure. The phrase next Bali does more harm than good here.
  • Singapore, Hong Kong or Bali?Both let foreigners own outright and both charge heavily for the privilege. Bali offers neither ownership nor the entry cost.
  • Is money moving from Dubai to Bali?Dubai prices rose hard and yields compressed. Some of that capital went looking, and Bali caught part of it. Most of it is buying the wrong thing.
  • What does your money actually buy in Bali?The same figure buys a different life here, and the gap is widest on exactly the things most people cannot buy at home any more.
  • Bali or Lombok for investment?Lombok is a fraction of the price and a fraction of the demand. The question is whether you are buying income or buying time.
  • Canggu, Seminyak or Ubud?Three markets with different guests, different booking channels and different seasonality. The right one depends on what you are optimising for.
  • The Bukit or Canggu?Canggu has the steadiest occupancy in Bali. The Bukit has the biggest view premium and the water problem. They suit different buyers.
