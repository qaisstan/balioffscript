You cannot own land in Bali.
Three legal routes say otherwise.

Leasehold, Hak Pakai, or HGB through a PT PMA. Everything else being sold to you is a nominee arrangement — void since 1960, and a criminal offence in Bali since February 2026.

    Pink lets you rent. Yellow does not. Green gets demolished. Check it yourself before you pay anything.

  • What taxes do you pay when buying property in Bali?

    Around 5% for the buyer on a titled transfer — and zero on a leasehold, which is a bigger deal than it sounds.

  • How much capital do you need for a PT PMA in 2026?

    The paid-up requirement dropped 75% in October 2025. The investment plan requirement did not.
