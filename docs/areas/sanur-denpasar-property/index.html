Is Sanur the best value in Bali right now?

Established, calmer, cheaper per square metre, and the fastest regency in Bali for permits.

Reference
Legal basis
Perda Denpasar RDTR; PP 16/2021
Applies to
Buyers looking at Sanur, Renon, Denpasar
Verified
2026-08-14

Sanur offers the best value per square metre of any established area in Bali. Older demographic, families, calm water, and a guest profile that stays longer and complains less.

The Kura Kura special economic zone sits next door as a medium-term catalyst.

The underrated advantage: permits actually move

Sanur is in Denpasar, which is the fastest jurisdiction in Bali for building approvals — better digital integration with the national SIMBG system, and a focus on residential and smaller commercial buildings rather than large resort projects.

SituationDenpasarBadung, for comparison
PBG from empty land3–5 months5–6 months
PBG + SLF for existing building8–10 months10–12 months

On a project where you are carrying finance or losing rental months, that difference is worth real money. It rarely appears in a comparison of land prices.

What Sanur is not

It is not a high-ADR market. Nightly rates are materially below Canggu and well below the Bukit. The case for Sanur is stable occupancy, lower entry cost, lower operating friction and easier resale — not headline yield.

If a projection shows Bukit nightly rates on Sanur land, the projection is wrong.

What to check

  • The RDTR designation for the parcel, as everywhere
  • PBG and SLF status for any existing structure
  • Whether the rental model is genuinely long-stay residential or short-term accommodation, because they are different legal activities with different licensing
  • Beach setback where relevant

Run the numbers

Sanur is the area where the honest arithmetic tends to look best, because entry cost is lower and the operating drag is smaller. Put your actual figures into the ROI calculator rather than trusting a comparison built on gross yield.
