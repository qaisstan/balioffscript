Visas

Which visa lets you come and check out a property properly?

There is a purpose-built pre-investment category. Most buyers use a tourist visa and run out of time.

Reference
Legal basis
C12 and D12 indices, Ditjen Imigrasi
Applies to
Buyers evaluating an Indonesian investment before committing
Verified
2026-08-14

Most people looking at Bali property arrive on a visa on arrival, spend thirty days trying to do due diligence properly, extend once, and leave with the work half done.

There is a category built for exactly this, and almost nobody uses it.

The pre-investment visas

IndexTypeDuration
C12Single-entry pre-investmentCommonly around 60 days, extendable
D12Multiple-entry pre-investment180 days, extendable by a further 180 — up to 12 months total

D12 is the useful one. Multiple entry, six months at a time, up to a year in total. It is designed for site visits, due diligence, and meetings with counsel and agents before committing to an investment.

For anyone seriously evaluating a purchase — walking plots, verifying certificates at BPN, sitting with a notary, checking zoning at the regency office — that is the difference between doing it properly and doing it in a rush.

What it does not authorise

Neither C12 nor D12 permits:

  • Signing on as an employee
  • Running a business day to day
  • Drawing Indonesian income

They are visitor categories. Once the investment is actually made, you move to the relevant E28 investor route.

Why this matters for the due diligence itself

The checks that actually protect you take time, and several cannot be rushed:

  • Certificate verification at the local BPN office through your own notary
  • Zoning and RDTR confirmation at the regency planning office
  • Confirming KDB and KLB for the specific parcel
  • Establishing every setback in writing
  • Confirming PBG and SLF status for anything already built
  • Verifying the operator's NIB and business classification

Two to four weeks is a realistic minimum for thorough due diligence, and that assumes offices respond promptly. A thirty-day tourist visa with a flight home at the end of it produces exactly the pressure that makes people skip steps.

If you are being told to move fast, that is the pressure working. Nothing legitimate in Indonesian land transfer requires you to skip a step.

The comparison

RouteRealistic for due diligence?
Visa exemption (under 30 days)No
Visa on arrival — 30 days + one extensionTight, and the extension eats a week
C12 single entryWorkable for a focused trip
D12 multiple entry, up to 12 monthsBuilt for this

If you are spending several hundred thousand dollars, the visa that lets you verify it properly costs a rounding error by comparison.
