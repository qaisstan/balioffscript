What permit can the parent of an Indonesian citizen get?

Family connection to an Indonesian citizen opens routes that are not available to unconnected foreign nationals.

By Kai, Bali property adviser Updated 2026-09-21 4 min read

Indonesia recognises family connection as a basis for residency, and the routes reach further than most people expect.

Who can sponsor

An Indonesian citizen can sponsor certain foreign family members for stay permits, and the category depends on the relationship.

A KITAS or KITAP holder can sponsor a spouse and children as dependants.

The strongest position is connection to an Indonesian citizen, because it does not depend on someone else's permit continuing.

The relationships that qualify

A foreign spouse of an Indonesian citizen, which is the most common and most established route, leading in time toward KITAP.

Foreign children of an Indonesian citizen — though where the child was born to a mixed marriage, the more important question is usually the dual citizenship position rather than a permit, because an Indonesian citizen child can hold Hak Milik freehold and a foreign one cannot.

Foreign parents of an Indonesian citizen. This is the one people do not know about. Where an adult Indonesian citizen has a foreign parent — through a mixed marriage, through a parent who naturalised elsewhere, or through adoption — there is a basis for the parent to obtain residency sponsored by the child.

Stepchildren and adopted children, with their own requirements and documentation.

Specific categories, index codes and conditions change. Verify the current position for the actual relationship rather than assuming from a general description.

The documents, which are the bottleneck

Every one of these depends on evidencing the relationship, and that is where applications stall.

Birth certificates, marriage certificates, adoption orders, naturalisation records.

Where documents were issued abroad, they need legalisation — by apostille, since Indonesia is a party to the Hague Convention — and sworn translation by a *penerjemah tersumpah*.

Where a marriage or birth occurred abroad and involved an Indonesian citizen, it generally must have been reported to the Indonesian representative in that country and registered here, within time limits. A relationship that was never reported is a relationship Indonesia does not formally recognise, which surfaces at the application.

This is the most common cause of delay and the reason to start months rather than weeks ahead.

What these permits do not do

They do not authorise work. Family-based permits grant residence. Employment with an Indonesian company still requires the employer to obtain RPTKA approval and a work permit.

There is a widely repeated belief that a family or spouse permit allows free working. Verify it against the current rules rather than acting on it, because the consequences fall on you and on your sponsor.

Why it matters for property

Residency makes Hak Pakai available — a registered right of use over property in your own name at the land office, subject to minimum value requirements.

That is the strongest ownership position open to a foreign individual, and it is unavailable to anyone without a KITAS or KITAP. Against an unregistered leasehold with a running term, it is a materially better instrument.

For a foreign parent of an Indonesian citizen intending to spend substantial time here, that is worth understanding before committing capital to a thirty year lease.

Separately: an Indonesian citizen — including your child — can hold Hak Milik freehold, which no foreigner can. For families with property here, that shapes inheritance planning in a way nothing else does.

The marital property agreement, again

If the family connection runs through a marriage between an Indonesian citizen and a foreign national, the prenuptial or postnuptial agreement is more consequential than any permit.

Without one, Indonesian joint marital property rules give the foreign spouse an interest in assets acquired during the marriage, and because a foreigner cannot hold Hak Milik, that compromises the Indonesian spouse's ability to hold freehold.

Since the Constitutional Court decision in 2015 it can be made after the wedding. Families sort permits and leave this, which is the wrong order.

If the sponsoring relationship ends

A permit sponsored by a relationship depends on that relationship. Divorce or death ends the basis, and there are rules about the period in which you must regularise or leave.

Take advice early rather than waiting, because arranging an alternative permit takes time.

Common questions

Can an Indonesian citizen sponsor a foreign parent for residency?

There is a basis for it, sponsored by the adult citizen child. Verify the current category and conditions, since immigration rules change.

Do family-based Indonesian permits allow work?

No. They grant residence. Employment with an Indonesian company still requires RPTKA approval and a work permit in your own name.

What documents prove a family relationship for an Indonesian permit?

Birth, marriage or adoption records, legalised by apostille where issued abroad and translated by a sworn translator, with foreign marriages and births also needing to have been reported and registered in Indonesia.

Does family residency let me buy property in Indonesia?

It makes Hak Pakai available, a registered right of use in your own name. Hak Milik freehold remains reserved to Indonesian citizens, including your child.

What happens if the sponsoring relationship ends?

The basis for the permit ends with it, and there are rules about the period in which you must regularise your status or leave. Take advice early.
