Can you study in Indonesia on a student visa?

Sponsored by the institution, not by you, and it does not permit work. The language-school route is the one foreigners in Bali actually use.

By Kai, Bali property adviser Updated 2026-09-20 2 min read

On this page

  1. How it works
  2. What it permits
  3. The Bahasa Indonesia route
  4. What to verify before enrolling
  5. What it does not lead to
  6. Common questions

The student route exists and is more relevant to Bali than people realise, because language schools use it.

How it works

The institution sponsors you. You do not apply independently. That means the school, university or training provider must be recognised and willing to act as sponsor.

A legitimate institution will have done this before and will know exactly what it needs from you.

What it permits

Study at the sponsoring institution. Residence for the duration of the course.

Not work. A student permit does not grant the right to earn, and the enforcement test applied since April 2026 is activity rather than payment. Teaching a class on the side, taking freelance work, or running anything commercial is a violation.

The Bahasa Indonesia route

The practical version for many foreigners in Bali is enrolling at a recognised language school. It provides a lawful basis to be here while you learn the language, which is genuinely useful.

Bahasa Indonesia is among the easier languages for English speakers: no tenses in the European sense, no gender, no cases, no tones, and a phonetic Latin alphabet. Useful conversational level is achievable in months.

What to verify before enrolling

  • Is the institution recognised and able to sponsor?
  • Have they sponsored foreign students recently, and can they say so specifically?
  • What is the attendance requirement, and is it enforced?
  • What happens to the permit if you stop attending?
  • Does the sponsorship cover the full period you intend to stay?

That attendance point matters. A permit granted for study and not used for study is a permit resting on a basis that no longer holds.

What it does not lead to

The student route is not a path to permanent stay in the way consecutive years on a work or investor KITAS can be. Treat it as a lawful way to be here for a defined purpose rather than a long-term residence strategy.

Common questions

Can foreigners study in Indonesia?

Yes, on a student permit sponsored by a recognised institution. You cannot apply independently of the school.

Can I work on an Indonesian student visa?

No. It grants residence for study, not the right to earn, and that distinction is being enforced.

Can I get a visa to learn Indonesian in Bali?

Recognised language schools can sponsor students, which is the route many foreigners here use.

Does a student visa lead to KITAP?

It is not a route to permanent stay in the way work or investor categories can be.

What if I stop attending the course?

The permit rests on the study basis. If that ends, so does the ground the permit was granted on.
