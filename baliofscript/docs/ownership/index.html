Ownership

What foreigners can actually own, and how the three legal routes really work.

  • Can a foreigner own property in Bali?

    Not freehold. But three legal structures exist, and one of them puts a certificate in your own name.

  • What makes a Bali leasehold extension clause actually safe?

    The initial term is not the asset. The extension clause is, and most of them are worthless.

  • What actually happens if you buy through a nominee?

    The agreement is void the day it is signed, and since February 2026 it is criminal in Bali.

  • How does Hak Pakai work, and who can actually get it?

    The one route that registers land in a foreigner's own name. Requires residency, and most people are never told it exists.
